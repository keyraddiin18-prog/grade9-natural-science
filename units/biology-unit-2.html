Part 1: Characteristics of Living Things (2.1)

Living things come in a wide variety of shapes and forms. All living things share common characteristics:

  • Made of cells: Unicellular (e.g., bacteria) or Multicellular (e.g., plants, animals).
  • Need Energy: For metabolic activities. Producers (Autotrophs) make their own food (e.g., plants). Consumers (Heterotrophs) obtain energy by consuming others.
  • Respond to stimuli: They detect changes in their environment and respond.
  • Growth: Single-celled grow by cell enlargement; multicellular grow by increasing cell numbers.
  • Reproduction: Making more of the same kind. Single-celled divide (asexual); multicellular may reproduce sexually or asexually.
  • Excretion: Removal of metabolic wastes (e.g., expulsion of carbon dioxide).
  • Ordered complexity: Highly complex and structured (simple molecules to complex cells).
  • Homeostasis: Maintenance of stable internal conditions (e.g., human body temperature regulation).
  • Adaptation: Evolve over time to survive in environments.

Part 2: Taxonomy of Living Things (2.2)

Taxonomy is the science of naming, identifying, and classifying organisms based on similarities, differences, structures, habitat, and genetic makeup.

Taxonomic Hierarchies

Aristotle developed the first system (plants: trees, shrubs, herbs; animals: red blood/no red blood). Later, Carolus Linnaeus (father of taxonomy) introduced a more organized system.

Hierarchy (Broadest to Most Specific):

  1. Domain
  2. Kingdom
  3. Phylum (Division)
  4. Class
  5. Order
  6. Family
  7. Genus
  8. Species (Most specific, can interbreed to produce fertile offspring).

Domains of Life

  • Bacteria: Single-celled, no nucleus (prokaryotic).
  • Archaea: Single-celled, no nucleus, similar to eukaryotes.
  • Eukarya: Cells with a nucleus (Protists, Fungi, Plants, Animals).

Part 3: Relevance of Classification (2.3)

Classification helps biologists study relationships between living (extant) and extinct organisms. Example: Similar bone structures in birds and dinosaurs suggest a closer relationship.

It is useful in agriculture, forestry, and medicine to discover new resources (lumber, food, medicines, energy).

Review Questions (Part 3)

    Answer: b) By discovering new resources like medicines

Part 4: Linnaean System of Nomenclature (2.4)

Carl Linnaeus published Systema Naturae in 1735 and popularized the Binomial system of naming.

  • Each scientific name has two parts: Genus (Capital letter) + Specific epithet (lowercase).
  • When handwritten: underlined. When printed: italicized (e.g., Homo sapiens).
  • Example: False banana (Ensete ventricosum), Banana (Musa acuminata). Both are in the family Musaceae.
  • Importance: Scientific names are recognized worldwide, avoiding confusion from local names (e.g., Maize is Bekkolo, Boqqollo, Baddela, corn, but scientifically Zea mays). Ethiopia has 80+ ethnolinguistic groups, making standard naming essential.

Part 6: Dichotomous Keys & The Five-Kingdom System (2.6)

Dichotomous Keys

Tools for identifying unfamiliar organisms using pairs of contrasting features. "Dichotomous" means two branches.

Example: Step 1: Poikilothermic (cold-blooded) or Homoiothermic (warm-blooded). This method classifies organisms into class, genus, or species.

The Five-Kingdom System

Proposed by Whittaker (1969) to solve the pitfalls of the two-kingdom system. Criteria: Cell structure, body organization, mode of nutrition, reproduction, evolutionary relationships.

  1. Monera: Prokaryotic, unicellular.
  2. Protista: Eukaryotic, mostly unicellular.
  3. Fungi: Eukaryotic, heterotrophic, chitin cell walls.
  4. Plantae: Multicellular, autotrophic, cellulose cell walls.
  5. Animalia: Multicellular, heterotrophic, no cell walls.

Note on Viruses: Viruses are not included in any kingdom because they do not meet the criteria for living organisms (lack growth, development, independent respiration). They depend entirely on host cells for replication.

Part 7: The Kingdoms (Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia)

Kingdom Monera

Includes Eubacteria and Archaebacteria.

  • Eubacteria: Strong cell walls, various shapes, heterotrophs or autotrophs (photosynthetic/chemosynthetic). Some cause diseases, most are harmless/beneficial.
  • Archaebacteria: Cell walls different, make their own food (chemo/photo), live in extreme environments (swamps, deep-ocean vents, oxygen-free).
  • Did you know? Only a small number of bacteria cause diseases. They harm by interfering with normal tissue function or releasing toxins.

Review Questions (Monera)

  1. What type of cells do organisms in Monera possess?
    Answer: b) Prokaryotic
  2. Which describes chemosynthetic bacteria?
    Answer: b) Obtain energy through chemical reactions
  3. What type of environments do archaebacteria thrive in?
    Answer: b) Extreme environments
  4. Organisms with prokaryotic type of cell are grouped in the kingdom:
    Answer: C. Monera
  5. Which belongs to kingdom Monera?
    Answer: E. Mycobacterium tuberculosis

Kingdom Protista

The most diverse kingdom, includes unicellular and multicellular organisms. All are eukaryotic. Types:

  • Protozoans: Animal-like, unicellular.
  • Algae: Plant-like, photosynthetic, lack roots/stems/leaves. Form the basis of aquatic food chains.
  • Fungus-like Protists: Resemble fungi but lack the same type of cell walls.

Review Questions (Protista)

  1. Which is an example of a plant-like protist?
    Answer: a) Algae
  2. What distinguishes protists from monera?
    Answer: b) They are eukaryotic
  3. Which type of protists form the basis of aquatic food chains?
    Answer: b) Plant-like protists
  4. Which is a member of Kingdom Protista?
    Answer: C. Plasmodium falciparum

Kingdom Fungi

Eukaryotic organisms including yeasts, molds, mushrooms. Unicellular or multicellular (made of hyphae). Cell walls made of chitin.

  • Nutrition: Heterotrophs that absorb nutrients. Release enzymes to break down food externally (extracellular digestion).
  • Types: Saprophytes (decomposers, feed on dead matter), Parasites (feed on living organisms, cause diseases), Mutualists (benefit both, e.g., fungi in termites, lichens, mycorrhiza).
  • Reproduction: Vegetative, Asexual (spores), Sexual (spores with variation). Yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) is used to make injera rise and produce Tej, Tella, beer.

Review Questions (Fungi)

  1. What substance is the cell wall of fungi made of?
    Answer: b) Chitin
  2. How do fungi obtain nutrients?
    Answer: b) Extracellular digestion
  3. Which type of fungi feeds on dead organic matter?
    Answer: c) Saprophytes

Kingdom Plantae

Eukaryotic, multicellular, autotrophic. Produce food via photosynthesis (chloroplasts, chlorophyll). Stationary. Cell walls made of cellulose.

  • Bryophytes: Non-vascular (mosses, liverworts).
  • Pteridophytes: Vascular, seedless (ferns).
  • Gymnosperms: Vascular, seeds not enclosed in ovary (conifers).
  • Angiosperms: Vascular, seeds enclosed in ovary, produce flowers and fruits. Most diverse (Monocots: one seed leaf; Dicots: two seed leaves).

Review Questions (Plantae)

  1. What process allows plants to produce their own food?
    Answer: b) Photosynthesis
  2. What are the specialized tissues in vascular plants?
    Answer: a) Xylem and phloem
  3. Which group produces flowers and fruits?
    Answer: b) Angiosperms
  4. Ferns are advanced compared to mosses because ferns:
    Answer: D. Are seedless vascular plants
  5. Which group lacks vascular tissue?
    Answer: H. Bryophytes
  6. The most dominant plants on earth are:
    Answer: J. angiosperms

Kingdom Animalia

Multicellular, heterotrophic, eukaryotic. Ingest food, lack cell walls (cells held by collagen). Have muscle and nerve cells. Reproduce sexually.

  • Invertebrates: No backbone.
  • Vertebrates: Have a backbone.

Review Questions (Animalia)

  1. What feature distinguishes animals from fungi?
    Answer: b) Ingestion of food
  2. Which group lacks a backbone?
    Answer: c) Invertebrates
  3. What structural protein is found only in animals?
    Answer: b) Collagen
  4. Which represents general characteristics of animals?
    Answer: B. Multicellular, heterotrophic, Eukaryotic

Kutaa 1: Amaloota Wantoota Lubbuu Qaban (2.1)

Wantoonni lubbuu qaban boca fi caasaa adda addaa qabu. Hunda isaaniif amaloota waliinii kanneen qabu:

  • Seelii irraa ijaaramuu: Seelii tokko qabu (fkn: baakteeriyaa) ykn seelii hedduu qabu (fkn: biqiltuu, bineensa).
  • Humna barbaaduu: Sochii metaboolizimii tiif. Oomishitoonni (Autotrophs) ofii nyaata isaanii oomishu (fkn: biqiltuu). Fayyadamtoonni (Heterotrophs) nyaata biraa nyaachuun humna argatu.
  • Kaka'umsaaf deebii kennuu: Jijjiirama naannoo isaanii hubatanii deebii kennu.
  • Guddina: Seelii tokko qaban baay'ina seelii isaatiin guddatu; seelii hedduu qaban baay'ina seelii dabaluun guddatu.
  • Walhormaata: Sanyii isaanii dabaluu. Seelii tokko qaban addaan ba'uun (asexual); seelii hedduu qaban saalaan ykn saala malee horachuu danda'u.
  • Xuuxuu (Excretion): Culluqa metaboolizimii (fkn: kaarboon daayooksaayidii) qaama keessaa baasuu.
  • Caasaa qindaa'e: Baay'ee xixiqqaa fi qindoomina qabu (molekiyulii salphaa irraa hanga seelii walxaxaatti).
  • Homeostasis: Haala naannoo keessoo tasgabbii qabu eeguu (fkn: ho'ina qaama namaa to'achuu).
  • Madaqinsa (Adaptation): Naannoo keessa jiraachuuf dhalootaa dhalootatti jijjiiramuu.

Gaaffiiwwan Shaakalaa (Kutaa 1)

  1. Amaloota wantoota lubbuu qaban keessaa kami?
    Deebii: d) Hunda isaanii
  2. Organiizimii akkamtu humna aduu fayyadamuun nyaata ofii isaa oomisha?
    Deebii: b) Oomishitoota (Producers)
  3. Jechi culluqa metaboolizimii baasuu ibsu maali?
    Deebii: b) Xuuxuu (Excretion)
  4. Homeostasis jechuun maali?
    Deebii: b) Haala naannoo keessoo tasgabbii qabu eeguu
  5. Organiizimii seelii tokko qabu fakkeenya isa kami?
    Deebii: a) Baakteeriyaa
  6. Organiizimoonni seelii hedduu qaban akkamitti guddatu?
    Deebii: b) Baay'ina seelii dabaluun

Kutaa 2: Saayinsii Ramaddii (Taxonomy) (2.2)

Taxonomy jechuun saayinsii maqaa kennuu, adda baasuu fi organiizimoota ramadduu dha. Kun amaloota, caasaa, bakka jireenyaa fi jeneetiksii irratti hundaa'a.

Sadarkaa Ramaddii

Aristotle sirna jalqabaa kaa'e. Booda, Carolus Linnaeus (abbaa taxonomy) sirna ammayyaa kaa'e.

Sadarkaa (Guddaa irraa gara Xixiqqaatti):

  1. Domain
  2. Kingdom (Mootummaa)
  3. Phylum (Division)
  4. Class (Garee)
  5. Order (Tartiiba)
  6. Family (Maatii)
  7. Genus (Sanyii guddaa)
  8. Species (Sanyii xixiqqaa) (Sanyii tokko keessatti walhormaata raawwatanii sanyii horachuu danda'u).

Domains of Life

  • Bacteria: Seelii tokko qabu, niwkiliyasii hin qabu (prokaryotic).
  • Archaea: Seelii tokko qabu, niwkiliyasii hin qabu, garuu eukaryotesitti dhihoo.
  • Eukarya: Seelii niwkiliyasii qabu (Protists, Fungi, Biqiltuu, Bineensa).

Gaaffiiwwan Shaakalaa (Kutaa 2)

  1. Eenyu abbaa taxonomy jedhamee beekama?
    Deebii: b) Carl Linnaeus
  2. Sadarkaa ramaddii bal'aan kami?
    Deebii: c) Domain
  3. Domain kam organiizimoota seelii niwkiliyasii qaban of keessatti qabata?
    Deebii: c) Eukarya
  4. Kaayyoon ramaddii inni guddaan maali?
    Deebii: b) Organiizimoota qindeessuu fi hubachuuf
  5. Jechi organiizimootaaf maqaa kennuu ibsu maali?
    Deebii: b) Nomenclature
  6. Tartiibni ramaddii sirrii kami?
    Deebii: D. Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species
  7. Uumama keessatti walhormaata raawwatanii sanyii horachuu dandeessu amaloota maalii?
    Deebii: H. Species
  8. Ramaddii genera hedduu of keessatti qabatu kami?
    Deebii: J. Family

Kutaa 4: Sirna Maqaa Linnaeus (2.4)

Carl Linnaeus kitaaba Systema Naturae (1735) barreessee, sirna Maqaa Kutaa Lamaa (Binomial) fayyadama.

  • Maqaan saayinsii kutaa lama qaba: Genus (Qubeen jalqabaa guddaa) + Specific epithet (Qubeen jalqabaa xiqqaa).
  • Yoo harkaan barreessinu: jala sararama. Yoo maxxanfamu: italic ta'a (fkn: Homo sapiens).
  • Fakkeenya: Qochoo (Ensete ventricosum), Muuzii (Musa acuminata). Lamaanuu maatii Musaceae keessa jiru.
  • Faayidaa: Maqaan saayinsii addunyaa mara irratti beekama, maqaa naannoo irraa maddu walitti makamuu dhoorkaa. Fakkeenyaaf: Boqqolloon Afaan Oromootiin, Bekkolo Amaariffaan, Baddela Sidamaafiin jedhama, garuu maqaan saayinsii isaa Zea mays waan ta'eef ifa ta'a.

  2. Maqaan saayinsii sirriitti barreeffame kami?
    Deebii: b) Zea mays
  3. Maqaan saayinsii maaliif barbaachisa?
    Deebii: a) Maqaa naannoo irraa maddu walitti makamuu dhoorkuuf

    Deebii: A. Qubee xiqqaadhaan

    Deebii: G. Maqaan genus qubee guddaa, species qubee xiqqaa qabaachuu qaba

Kutaa 6: Furtuu Diikootoomii fi Sirna Mootummaa Shan (2.6)

Furtuu Diikootoomii (Dichotomous Keys)

Meeshaa organiizimoota hin beekamne adda baasuuf gargaaru. "Dichotomous" jechuun damee lama (filannoo lama) qabu jechuudha. Fakkeenyaaf: Ho'ina dhiigaa (Poikilothermic) ykn Ho'ina qaamaa (Homoiothermic) filachuu.

Sirna Mootummaa Shan (Five-Kingdom System)

Whittaker (1969) sirna mootummaa lamaanii fooyyessuuf kaa'e. Ijoo isaa: Caasaa seelii, qindoomina qaamaa, mala nyaata argachuu, walhormaata fi hariiroo madaqinsaa.

  1. Monera: Prokaryotic, seelii tokko qabu.
  2. Protista: Eukaryotic, irra caalaa seelii tokko qabu.
  3. Fungi: Eukaryotic, heterotrophic, dallaa seelii chitin qabu.
  4. Plantae: Seelii hedduu qabu, autotrophic, dallaa seelii cellulose qabu.
  5. Animalia: Seelii hedduu qabu, heterotrophic, dallaa seelii hin qaban.

Hubachiisa Vaayirasii: Vaayirasii mootummaa kamiyyuu keessa hin galmooftu sababiin isaa amaloota lubbuu (guddina, walhormaata of danda'aa) hin guutu. Seelii keessatti qofa jiraachuu danda'u.

Gaaffiiwwan Shaakalaa (Kutaa 6)

  1. Furtuu diikootoomii fayyadamuun tarkaanfiin kami?
    Deebii: b) Amaloota lama adda ta'an gidduu filachuu
  2. Faayidaan furtuu diikootoomii inni guddaan maali?
    Deebii: b) Organiizimoota hin beekamne adda baasuu
  3. Sirni ramaddii ammayyaa keessatti baay'ee fayyadamu kami?
    Deebii: D. Sirna mootummaa shan
  4. Ibsi vaayirasii hundaan sirrii ta'e kami?
    Deebii: E. Organiizimoota biraa keessa jiraatu

Kutaa 7: Mootummaawwan (Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia)

Mootummaa Monera

Eubacteria fi Archaebacteria of keessatti qabata.

  • Eubacteria: Dallaa seelii cimaa, boca adda addaa, heterotrophs ykn autotrophs. Baay'een isaanii faayidaa qabu, muraasni dhukkuba fidu.
  • Archaebacteria: Dallaa seelii adda, nyaata ofii oomishu, naannoo cimaa (bishaan waraan, lafa ho'aa) keessa jiraatu.

Gaaffiiwwan Shaakalaa (Monera)

  1. Organiizimoonni Monera seelii akkamii qabu?
    Deebii: b) Prokaryotic
  2. Baakteeriyaa kemosaayinthesisii akkamitti ibsina?
    Deebii: b) Sochii keemikaalaan humna argatu
  3. Archaebacteria naannoo akkamii keessa jiraatu?
    Deebii: b) Naannoo cimaa (Extreme environments)
  4. Organiizimoonni seelii prokaryotic qaban mootummaa kamitti ramadamu?
    Deebii: C. Monera
  5. Mootummaa Monera keessaa kami?
    Deebii: E. Mycobacterium tuberculosis

Mootummaa Protista

Mootummaa baay'ee bal'aa fi adda addaa. Hundi isaanii eukaryotic. Gosa isaa:

  • Protozoans: Bineensa fakkaatan, seelii tokko qabu.
  • Algae: Biqiltuu fakkaatan, photosynthesis godhu, hundee/hidda hin qaban. Nyaata bishaan keessaa ijoo ta'u.
  • Fungus-like Protists: Faanjii fakkaatan garuu dallaa seelii adda qabu.

Gaaffiiwwan Shaakalaa (Protista)

  1. Fakkeenyi protist biqiltuu fakkaatu kami?
    Deebii: a) Algae
  2. Protista Monera irraa maaliin adda ba'a?
    Deebii: b) Eukaryotic ta'uun
  3. Protist kamtu bu'uura nyaata bishaan keessaa ta'a?
    Deebii: b) Biqiltuu fakkaatan (Plant-like)
  4. Miseensi Mootummaa Protista kami?
    Deebii: C. Plasmodium falciparum

Mootummaa Fungi (Faanjii)

Organiizimoota eukaryotic kan akka raayistii, maalldii fi mushroom of keessatti qabatu. Dallaan seelii isaanii chitin irraa hojjetame.

  • Nyaata: Heterotrophs. Nyaata isaanii ala seelii isaatti (extracellular) enzymiin baasanii bulbulanii xuuxu.
  • Gosa: Saprophytes (wantoota du'an irraa nyaachu), Parasites (lubbuu qaban irraa nyaachu), Mutualists (lamaaniif faayidaa qaba, fkn: lichens, mycorrhiza).
  • Walhormaata: Spores uumuun. Raayistiin (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) buddeena ol kaasuuf fi farsoo (Tej, Tella, beer) hojjechuuf gargaara.

Gaaffiiwwan Shaakalaa (Fungi)

  1. Dallaan seelii faanjii maal irraa hojjetame?
    Deebii: b) Chitin
  2. Faanjiin nyaata akkamitti argata?
    Deebii: b) Extracellular digestion (Ala seelii bulbuluun)
  3. Faanjii akkamtu wantoota du'an irraa nyaata?
    Deebii: c) Saprophytes

Mootummaa Plantae (Biqiltoota)

Eukaryotic, seelii hedduu, autotrophic. Photosynthesis fayyadamuun nyaata oomishu. Dallaan seelii cellulose irraa hojjetame.

  • Bryophytes: Vaaskulaarii hin qaban (mosses).
  • Pteridophytes: Vaaskulaarii qabu, sanyii hin qaban (ferns).
  • Gymnosperms: Vaaskulaarii qabu, sanyiin isaanii ovarii keessa hin galmoofne (conifers).
  • Angiosperms: Vaaskulaarii qabu, sanyii fi daraaraa qabu. Baay'ee bal'aa (Monocots fi Dicots).

Gaaffiiwwan Shaakalaa (Plantae)

  1. Biqiltoonni nyaata isaanii akkamitti oomishu?
    Deebii: b) Photosynthesis
  2. Biqiltoota vaaskulaarii qaban keessatti maal akka tissues jedhamu?
    Deebii: a) Xylem fi phloem
  3. Gareen biqiltootaa kam daraaraa fi fuduraa oomisha?
    Deebii: b) Angiosperms
  4. Ferns mosses caalaa sadarkaa olaanaa qabu sababni isaa:
    Deebii: D. Vaaskulaarii qabaatanii sanyii hin qaban
  5. Gareen vaaskulaarii hin qabne kami?
    Deebii: H. Bryophytes
  6. Biqiltoonni lafa irratti baay'ee filatamoo ta'an:
    Deebii: J. Angiosperms

Mootummaa Animalia (Bineensota)

Seelii hedduu, heterotrophic, eukaryotic. Nyaata liqimsuun (ingestion) nyaatu, dallaa seelii hin qaban (seelii isaanii collageniin wal qabatu). Seelii maashii fi seelii narvii qabu. Saalaan walhormaatu.

  • Invertebrates: Laafaa (backbone) hin qaban.
  • Vertebrates: Laafaa qabu.

Gaaffiiwwan Shaakalaa (Animalia)

  1. Bineensonni faanjii irraa maaliin adda ba'u?
    Deebii: b) Nyaata liqimsuun nyaachuu isaaniitiin
  2. Gareen laafaa hin qabne kami?
    Deebii: c) Invertebrates
  3. Pirotiiniin caasaa bineensota qofa keessa argamu kami?
    Deebii: b) Collagen
  4. Amaloota waliigalaa Animalia kami?
    Deebii: B. Seelii hedduu, heterotrophic, Eukaryotic
