1. Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, students will be able to:

  • Identify the characteristics of living things.
  • Explain the principles of taxonomy and classification.
  • Describe the taxonomic hierarchy (Domain to Species).
  • Apply the rules of binomial nomenclature.
  • Use dichotomous keys to identify organisms.
  • Describe the five kingdoms of life.

2. Introduction to Classification

Living things come in a wide variety of shapes and forms. Biologists study them by observing wildlife, collecting fossils, and analyzing behavior. To organize this vast diversity, scientists use classification systems.

3. Detailed Notes

Part 1: Characteristics of Living Things

  • Organization: Made of one or more cells (Unicellular or Multicellular).
  • Energy: Producers (Autotrophs) make their own food; Consumers (Heterotrophs) eat others.
  • Response to Stimuli: Detect changes and respond.
  • Growth: Increase in cell size or number.
  • Reproduction: Producing new organisms (Asexual or Sexual).
  • Excretion: Removal of metabolic wastes (e.g., CO2).
  • Ordered Complexity: Highly structured from simple molecules to complex cells.
  • Homeostasis: Maintaining stable internal conditions (e.g., body temperature).
  • Adaptation: Evolving over time to survive.

Part 2: Taxonomy and Hierarchy

Taxonomy: The science of naming, identifying, and classifying organisms based on similarities and differences.

Taxonomic Hierarchy (Broadest to Specific): Domain → Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species.

  • Domains of Life: Bacteria (no nucleus), Archaea (no nucleus, extreme environments), Eukarya (has a nucleus).

Part 3: Relevance and Nomenclature

  • Relevance: Helps study relationships between living and extinct organisms, useful in agriculture, forestry, and medicine.
  • Binomial Nomenclature: Developed by Carolus Linnaeus. Each organism has a two-part scientific name: Genus (capitalized) + specific epithet (lowercase). Italicized when printed, underlined when handwritten (e.g., Homo sapiens, Zea mays).

Part 4: Common Ethiopian Animals and Plants

Ethiopia is a biodiversity hotspot. Endemic crops include Noug (Guizotia abyssinica), Tef (Eragrostis tef), and Ethiopian mustard. It is also a route for domestic animals moving from Asia to Africa.

Part 5: Dichotomous Keys & Five Kingdoms

  • Dichotomous Keys: Tools using pairs of contrasting features (two branches) to identify organisms.
  • Five Kingdoms (Whittaker, 1969): Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia.
  • Viruses: Not in any kingdom. They lack characteristics of life (no growth, independent respiration) and depend entirely on host cells.

Part 6: The Kingdoms

  • Monera: Prokaryotic, unicellular (Eubacteria, Archaebacteria).
  • Protista: Eukaryotic, mostly unicellular (Protozoans, Algae, Fungus-like).
  • Fungi: Eukaryotic, heterotrophic, cell walls of chitin (yeasts, molds, mushrooms).
  • Plantae: Eukaryotic, multicellular, autotrophic, cell walls of cellulose (Bryophytes, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms, Angiosperms).
  • Animalia: Eukaryotic, multicellular, heterotrophic, no cell walls (Invertebrates and Vertebrates).

Part 7: Renowned Taxonomists in Ethiopia

5. Summary

  • Living things share common characteristics like organization, energy use, growth, and homeostasis.
  • Classification organizes organisms into a hierarchy from Domain to Species.
  • Binomial nomenclature provides a universal naming system using Genus and species.
  • Whittaker's Five-Kingdom system divides life based on cell structure, body organization, and nutrition.

1. Kaayyoo Barnootaa (Learning Objectives)

Erga kutaa kana xumurani booda, barattoonni:

  • Amaloota wantoota lubbuu qaban adda baasuu ni danda'u.
  • Sirna ramaddii fi taxonomy ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Sadarkaa ramaddii (Domain hanga Species) ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Seera maqaa saayinsii (binomial nomenclature) hojiitti hiikuu ni danda'u.
  • Furtuu diikootoomii (dichotomous keys) fayyadamuun organiizimoota adda baasuu ni danda'u.
  • Mootummaa shan lubbuu (Five Kingdoms) ibsuu ni danda'u.

2. Seensa Ramaddii (Introduction)

Wantoonni lubbuu qaban boca fi caasaa adda addaa qabu. Ogeeyyiin Biology qorannoo bineensotaa, fossilii fi amala isaanii ilaaluun isaan qoratu. Baay'ina kana qindeessuuf, saayintistoonni sirna ramaddii (classification) fayyadamu.

3. Yaadannoo Barnootaa (Detailed Notes)

Kutaa 1: Amaloota Wantoota Lubbuu Qaban

  • Qindoomina: Seelii tokko ykn seelii hedduu irraa ijaaramuu (Unicellular ykn Multicellular).
  • Humna: Oomishitoonni (Autotrophs) nyaata ofii oomishu; Fayyadamtoonni (Heterotrophs) kan biraa nyaatu.
  • Deebii Kaka'umsaaf Kennuu: Jijjiirama hubatanii deebii kennu.
  • Guddina: Baay'ina ykn hammi seelii dabaluu.
  • Walhormaata: Organiizimii haaraa dhalachuu (Asexual ykn Sexual).
  • Xuuxuu (Excretion): Culluqa metaboolizimii (fkn: CO2) qaama keessaa baasuu.
  • Caasaa qindaa'e: Molekiyulii salphaa irraa hanga seelii walxaxaatti qindaa'uu.
  • Homeostasis: Haala naannoo qaama keessoo tasgabbii qabu eeguu (fkn: ho'ina qaamaa).
  • Madaqinsa: Naannoo keessa jiraachuuf dhalootaa dhalootatti jijjiiramuu.

Kutaa 2: Taxonomy fi Sadarkaa Ramaddii

Taxonomy: Saayinsii maqaa kennuu, adda baasuu fi organiizimoota amala isaaniitiin ramadduu.

Sadarkaa Ramaddii (Guddaa irraa gara Xixiqqaatti): Domain → Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species.

  • Domains Sadan: Bacteria (niwkiliyasii hin qabu), Archaea (niwkiliyasii hin qabu, naannoo cimaa), Eukarya (niwkiliyasii qaba).

Kutaa 3: Faayidaa fi Maqaa Saayinsii

  • Faayidaa: Hariiroo organiizimoota jiranii fi dhumatan qorachuuf, qonnaa, bosonaa fi yaalaaf ni gargaara.
  • Binomial Nomenclature: Carolus Linnaeus irraa dhufe. Maqaan saayinsii kutaa lama qaba: Genus (qubee guddaan jalqaba) + species (qubee xiqqaan jalqaba). Yoo maxxanfamu italic, yoo harkaan barreeffamu jala sararama (fkn: Homo sapiens, Zea mays).

Kutaa 4: Biqiltoota fi Bineensota Itoophiyaa

Itoophiyaan biodiversity guddaa qabdi. Biqiltoota daangaa keessatti argaman: Nuugii (Guizotia abyssinica), Xaafii (Eragrostis tef), Shumburaa Itoophiyaa. Kanaan booda karaa bineensota manaa Asiyaa irraa gara Afrikaatti ce'an taateetti tajaajilteetti.

Kutaa 5: Furtuu Diikootoomii fi Mootummaa Shan

  • Furtuu Diikootoomii: Meeshaa amaloota faallaa ta'aniin (damee lama) organiizimoota adda baasuuf gargaaru.
  • Mootummaa Shan (Whittaker, 1969): Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia.
  • Vaayirasii: Mootummaa kamiyyuu keessa hin jiru. Amaloota lubbuu (guddina, respiration of danda'aa) waan hin qabneef, seelii keessatti qofa jiraatu.

Kutaa 6: Mootummaawwan (Kingdoms)

  • Monera: Prokaryotic, seelii tokko (Eubacteria, Archaebacteria).
  • Protista: Eukaryotic, irra caalaa seelii tokko (Protozoans, Algae, kan faanjii fakkaatu).
  • Fungi: Eukaryotic, heterotrophic, dallaa seelii chitin qabu (raayistii, maalldii, mushroom).
  • Plantae: Eukaryotic, seelii hedduu, autotrophic, dallaa seelii cellulose qabu (Bryophytes, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms, Angiosperms).
  • Animalia: Eukaryotic, seelii hedduu, heterotrophic, dallaa seelii hin qabu (Invertebrates fi Vertebrates).

Kutaa 7: Ogeeyyii Taxonomy Itoophiyaa

Dr. Mesfin Taddese, Prof. Sebsebe Demissew, Prof. Ensermu Kelbessa, fi Prof. Silesh Nemomissa kitaaba Flora of Ethiopia keessatti gumaacha guddaa qabu.

5. Cuunfaa (Summary)

  • Wantoonni lubbuu qaban amaloota waliinii akka qindoomina, humna, guddina fi homeostasis qabu.
  • Ramaddiin organiizimoota sadarkaa Domain hanga Species qindeessa.
  • Binomial nomenclature sirna maqaa addunyaa Genus fi species fayyadamu dha.
  • Sirni Mootummaa Shan caasaa seelii, qindoomina qaamaa fi mala nyaata irratti hundaa'a.
