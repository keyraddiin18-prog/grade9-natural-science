1. Definition of Biology

The term "biology" is derived from the Greek words "bios" (meaning life) and "logos" (meaning study or science). Biology is the scientific study of life or living things.

Characteristics of Living Things:

  • Organization: Complex structure made of cells.
  • Response to Stimuli: Respond to environmental changes.
  • Growth and Development: Grow according to DNA instructions.
  • Reproduction: Ability to produce new offspring.
  • Heredity: Passing genetic traits to the next generation.
  • Metabolism: Chemical reactions to transform energy.
  • Homeostasis: Regulating internal environment to stay stable.
  • Adaptation through Evolution: Evolving over generations to fit the environment.

2. The Scientific Method

  1. Observation: Noticing a phenomenon or problem.
  2. Asking Questions: Formulating a clear question.
  3. Forming a Hypothesis: Proposing a testable explanation.
  4. Experiment: Designing a controlled test.
  5. Data Collection: Gathering results.
  6. Analysis: Interpreting the data.
  7. Conclusion: Deciding if the hypothesis was correct.
  8. Communication: Sharing results.

3. Laboratory Tools & Microscope

Microscope: Used to study objects too small to be seen with the naked eye (e.g., bacteria, viruses).

  • Light Microscope: Uses a beam of light (magnifies up to 2000X).
  • Electron Microscope: Uses a beam of electrons (magnifies up to 2,000,000X). Used for viruses.

Other Tools: Hand lens, Glass slides, Autoclave (sterilizes), Incubator (maintains temperature), Petri dishes, Flasks, Balance (measures mass), Beaker, Thermometer, Bunsen burner, etc.

4. Handling a Light Microscope

  • Carry properly using Base and Arm.
  • Always focus on low power (4x) first using the Coarse adjustment knob.
  • Use Fine adjustment knob to sharpen the focus.
  • Clean lenses with lens paper and alcohol.

5. Laboratory Safety Rules

  • Never eat or drink in the laboratory.
  • Never taste or sniff chemicals.
  • Always follow instructions.
  • Know the location of safety equipment.
  • Clean your experiment area after each experiment.

1. Hiika Saayinsii Lubbuu (Biology)

Jechi "Biology" jedhu jechoota Giriikii "bios" (lubbuu) fi "logos" (qorannoo ykn saayinsii) irraa dhufe. Biology qorannoo saayinsii lubbuu ykn wantoota lubbuu qabanii ti.

Amaloota Wantoota Lubbuu Qaban:

  • Qindoomina (Organization): Caasaa xixiqqaa seelii irraa ijaarame qabaatu.
  • Deebii kaka'umsaaf kennuu (Response to Stimuli): Jijjiirama naannootiif deebii kennuu.
  • Guddina fi Misooma (Growth & Development): Qajeelfama DNA isaanii hordofuun guddachuu.
  • Walhormaata (Reproduction): Dhaloota haaraa horachuu.
  • Dhaala (Heredity): Amaloota jeneetiksii dhalootaa dhalootaatti dabarsuu.
  • Metabolizimii (Metabolism): Humna jijjiiruuf sochii keemikaalaa raawwachuu.
  • To'annoo naannoo keessoo (Homeostasis): Haala qaama isaanii tasgabbii qabu eeguuf to'achuu.
  • Madaqinsa (Adaptation through Evolution): Dhalootaa dhalootaatti naannoo isaaniitti madaquuf jijjiiramuu.

2. Mala Saayinsii (The Scientific Method)

  1. Qorannoo/Mul'achuu (Observation): Mudaa ykn rakkoo tokko hubachuu.
  2. Gaaffii gaafachuu (Asking Questions): Gaaffii ifa ta'e dhiyeessuu.
  3. Yaada dhiyeessuu (Hypothesis): Ibsa qoratamuu danda'u dhiyeessuu.
  4. Eksperimentii (Experiment): Qorannoo to'atamaa qopheessuu fi raawwachuu.
  5. Odeeffannoo sassaabuu (Data Collection): Bu'aa qorannoo walitti qabuu.
  6. Xiinxaluu (Analysis): Odeeffannoo argame xiinxaluu.
  7. Murtee kennuu (Conclusion): Yaadni dhiyaate sirrii ture moo hin turre murteessuu.
  8. Gabaasuu (Communication): Bu'aa argame namoota biraatiif ibsuu/gabaasuu.

3. Meeshaalee Labaraatorii fi Maaykirooskooppii

Maaykirooskooppii: Wantoota ijaan ilaalamanii hin dandeenye (fkn: baakteeriyaa, vaayirasii) qorachuuf gargaara.

  • Maaykirooskooppii Ifa (Light Microscope): Ifa fayyadama (hanga 2000X guddisee agarsiisa).
  • Maaykirooskooppii Elektiroonii (Electron Microscope): Elektiroonii fayyadama (hanga 2,000,000X guddisee agarsiisa). Vaayirasii qorachuuf gargaara.

Meeshaalee Biroo: Hand lens (guddisee ilaaluu), Glass slides (seelii kaa'uu), Autoclave (baakteeriyaa ajjeesu), Incubator (ho'ina eeguu), Petri dishes, Flasks, Balance (ulfina safaruu), Beaker, Thermometer, Bunsen burner fi kkf.

4. Maaykirooskooppii Qabachuu fi Itti Fayyadamuu

  • Bu'uura (Base) fi Harka (Arm) isaa qabatanii sirriitti baatu.
  • Yeroo hunda humna gabaabaa (4x) irraa jalqabaa, coarse adjustment knob fayyadamuun focus godhaa.
  • Fine adjustment knob fayyadamuun suuraa ifa godhaa.
  • Leensii isaa waraqaa leensii fi alkooliin qulqulleessaa.

5. Seera Nageenya Labaraatorii

  • Labaraatorii keessatti gonkumaa hin nyaatin, hin dhugin.
  • Keemikaalota gonkumaa hin dhandhamin, hin urgoofin.
  • Yeroo hunda qajeelfama hordofaa.
  • Bakka meeshaaleen nageenyaa jiran beekaa.
  • Erga eksperimentiin xumurame booda iddoo hojii kee qulqulleessi.
