1. Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, students will be able to:

  • Differentiate between asexual and sexual reproduction.
  • Describe various methods of asexual reproduction (Fission, Budding, Vegetative propagation, etc.).
  • Identify male and female reproductive structures and their functions.
  • Explain the changes during puberty and the role of hormones.
  • Describe the menstrual cycle, fertilization, and pregnancy.
  • List methods of birth control and sexually transmitted infections (STIs).

2. Introduction

Reproduction is the biological process by which parents produce offspring, ensuring the continuation of the species. It can be Asexual (one parent, identical offspring) or Sexual (two parents, genetic variation).

3. Detailed Notes

Part 1: Asexual Reproduction

  • Fission: Division into two or more equal parts.
    - Binary fission: Parent cell divides into two identical cells (e.g., Bacteria).
    - Multiple fission: Nucleus divides repeatedly into many cells (e.g., Malarial parasite).
  • Fragmentation: Parent breaks into parts that grow into new individuals (e.g., Fungi, Spirogyra).
  • Budding: Outgrowth forms and pinches off (e.g., Yeast, Hydra).
  • Sporulation: Formation of spores (e.g., Fungi).
  • Vegetative Propagation: New plants from roots, stems, or leaves.
    - Natural: Stolons (Strawberry), Rhizome (Couch grass), Corm (Gladiolus), Tubers (Potato), Bulb (Onion).
    - Artificial: Grafting (Scion + Stock), Cutting (Hibiscus), Layering.
  • Parthenogenesis: Unfertilized egg develops into an organism (e.g., Drones in honeybees).

Part 2: Sexual Reproduction & Puberty

  • Primary characteristics: Present at birth (Penis, Testis; Vagina, Uterus, Ovaries).
  • Secondary characteristics: Develop during puberty (Body hair, muscle mass, deep voice in males; breasts, wide hips, menstruation in females).
  • Hormones: FSH and LH (from pituitary) control ovaries/testes. Testosterone drives male traits; Estrogen drives female traits.

Part 3: Male Reproductive System

  • Testes: In scrotum, produce sperm and testosterone.
  • Epididymis: Sperm maturation.
  • Vas deferens: Transports sperm to urethra.
  • Accessory Glands: Seminal vesicles (fructose energy), Prostate (alkaline fluid), Bulbourethral (lubrication).
  • Sperm: Head (DNA + enzyme), Midpiece (mitochondria), Tail (swimming).

Part 4: Female Reproductive System

  • Ovaries: Produce eggs (ova) and hormones (Estrogen, Progesterone).
  • Oviduct: Site of fertilization. Cilia move the egg.
  • Uterus: Houses the embryo. Lining is endometrium.
  • Ovarian Cycle: Follicular phase (days 1-14), Ovulation (day 14), Luteal phase (days 15-28, forms corpus luteum).
  • Menstrual Cycle: Flow phase, Follicular, Ovulation, Luteal.
  • Menopause: End of menstrual cycles (ages 45-50).

Part 5: Fertilization & Pregnancy

  • Fertilization: Fusion of sperm and egg in the oviduct.
  • Implantation: Embryo attaches to the uterine lining.
  • Twins: Identical (one egg splits) vs. Fraternal (two eggs, two sperm).
  • Placenta: Exchanges nutrients/oxygen between mother and embryo. Connected by umbilical cord. Amnion protects with fluid.

Part 6: Birth Control & STIs

  • Birth Control: Natural (Abstinence, Fertility awareness, Withdrawal) and Artificial (Condoms, IUD, Pills, Sterilization).
  • STIs: Trichomoniasis (parasite), Chlamydia (bacteria), Gonorrhea (bacteria), Syphilis (bacteria), HIV/AIDS (virus).

5. Summary

  • Reproduction can be asexual (rapid, identical) or sexual (genetic variation).
  • Puberty brings physical and psychological changes driven by hormones (Testosterone, Estrogen).
  • Fertilization occurs in the oviduct, leading to implantation in the uterus.
  • Family planning and understanding STIs are crucial for reproductive health.

1. Kaayyoo Barnootaa (Learning Objectives)

Erga kutaa kana xumurani booda, barattoonni:

  • Walhormaata saala malee fi saalaan gidduu garaagarummaa adda baasuu ni danda'u.
  • Mala walhormaata saala malee adda addaa (Fission, Budding, Vegetative propagation, fi kkf) ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Qaama walhormaata dhiiraa fi dubartii fi hojii isaanii adda baasuu ni danda'u.
  • Jijjiirama yeroo guddinaa (puberty) fi gahee hormoonii ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Mar'ummaa, fertilization, fi ulfaa ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Mala to'annoo da'umsaa (birth control) fi dhukkuboota saalaan daddarban (STIs) tarreessuu ni danda'u.

2. Seensa (Introduction)

Walhormaanni adeemsa maatiin sanyii (daa'imma) uumuudha, kunis gosa lubbuu itti fufsiisa. Walhormaanni Saala Malee (maatii tokko, sanyii wal fakkaatu) ykn Saalaan (maatii lama, jeneetikii adda addaa) ta'uu danda'a.

3. Yaadannoo Barnootaa (Detailed Notes)

Kutaa 1: Walhormaata Saala Malee

  • Fission (Addaan ba'uu): Kutaa lama ykn isaa ol ta'een adda ba'uu.
    - Binary fission: Seeliin haadhaa seelii intalaa lama wal fakkaatanitti adda ba'u (fkn: Baakteeriyaa).
    - Multiple fission: Niwkiliyasiin irra deddeebi'ee adda ba'ee seelii intalaa hedduu uuma (fkn: Paraasayitii malariaa).
  • Fragmentation (Caccabuu): Maatiin adda ba'ee, kutaan tokkoon tokkoon isaa organiizimii haaraa uuma (fkn: Faanjii, Spirogyra).
  • Budding (Dammaquu): Qaama maatii irraa wanti damee fakkaatu uumame adda ba'ee nama haaraa uuma (fkn: Raayistii, Hydra).
  • Sporulation: Uumama seelii walhormaataa spoorii jedhamuun (fkn: Faanjii).
  • Vegetative Propagation (Facaasuu biqiltuu): Hidda, jirma, ykn baala irraa biqiltuu haaraa uumama.
    - Uumamaa (Natural): Stolons (Strawberry), Rhizome (Couch grass), Corm (Gladiolus), Tubers (Dinnicha), Bulb (Qullubbii).
    - Nam-tolchee (Artificial): Grafting (Scion + Stock walitti qabuu), Cutting (Hibiscus), Layering.
  • Parthenogenesis: Ova (egg) fertilize hin taane irraa organiizimii uumuu (fkn: Kanniisa keessatti drone uumama).

Kutaa 2: Walhormaata Saalaan fi Guddina (Puberty)

  • Amaloota saalaa jalqabaa (Primary): Dhalootaan qabaachuu (Qaama saalaa, Testis; Vagina, Uterus, Ovarii).
  • Amaloota saalaa lammaffaa (Secondary): Yeroo guddina keessatti guddatu (Dhiira: Rifeensa qaamaa, maashii, sagalee gadi aanaa; Dubartii: Harma, qeensa bal'aa, mar'ummaa).
  • Hormoonii: FSH fi LH (pituitary irraa) ovarii fi testis to'atu. Testosterone amala dhiiraa, Estroojinii amala dubartii guddisa.

Kutaa 3: Sirna Walhormaata Dhiiraa

  • Testes: Scrotum keessa, spermii fi testosterone oomisha.
  • Epididymis: Bakka spermii bilchina argatu.
  • Vas deferens: Spermii gara urethra geessu.
  • Qaama Dabalataa (Accessory Glands): Seminal vesicles (humna fructose), Prostate (alkaline), Bulbourethral (lubrication).
  • Spermii: Mataa (DNA + enzyme), Midpiece (mitochondria), eegee (swimming).

Kutaa 4: Sirna Walhormaata Dubartii

  • Ovarii: Eggii fi hormoonii (Estroojinii, Progesterone) oomisha.
  • Oviduct: Bakka fertilization raawwatamu. Cilia eggii dhiibdi.
  • Uterus: Embryo qabata. Keessaan isaa endometrium jedhama.
  • Ovarian Cycle: Follicular phase (guyyaa 1-14), Ovulation (guyyaa 14), Luteal phase (guyyaa 15-28, corpus luteum uumama).
  • Menstrual Cycle: Flow phase, Follicular, Ovulation, Luteal.
  • Menopause: Xumura mar'ummaa (waggaa 45-50).

Kutaa 5: Fertilization fi Ulfaa

  • Fertilization: Spermii fi eggii oviduct keessatti walitti makamuu.
  • Implantation: Embryo keessatti endometrium keessa qubatu.
  • Lakkuu (Twins): Identical (eggii tokko adda ba'ee) vs. Fraternal (eggii lama, spermii lama).
  • Placenta: Nyaata/oksijiinii haadha fi daa'imaa gidduu jijjiiru. Umbilical cordiin hidhama. Amnion dhangala'aan qaama eegu.

Kutaa 6: To'annoo Da'umsaa fi Dhukkuboota Saalaan Daddarban

  • To'annoo Da'umsaa (Birth Control): Uumamaa (Abstinence, Fertility awareness, Withdrawal) fi Nam-tolchee (Condom, IUD, Qoricha, Sterilization).
  • STIs: Trichomoniasis (paraasayitii), Chlamydia (baakteeriyaa), Gonorrhea (baakteeriyaa), Syphilis (baakteeriyaa), HIV/AIDS (vaayirasii).

5. Cuunfaa (Summary)

  • Walhormaanni saala malee (saffisaa, wal fakkaataa) ykn saalaan (jeneetikii adda addaa) ta'uu danda'a.
  • Guddinni (Puberty) jijjiirama qaamaa fi sammuu hormoonii (Testosterone, Estroojinii) irraa ka'u fida.
  • Fertilization oviduct keessatti raawwatama, kunis gara uterusitti implantation ta'a.
  • To'annoon da'umsaa fi hubannoon STIs fayyaa walhormaataaf baay'ee barbaachisaa dha.
