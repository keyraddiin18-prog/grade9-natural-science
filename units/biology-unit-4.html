1. Introduction to Reproduction

Reproduction is the process by which parents produce offspring, ensuring the continuation of the species.

  • Asexual Reproduction: Offspring are produced by one parent only. No special reproductive organs/cells. Produces identical copies. Advantages: No need for two sex cells, offspring inherit favourable traits, ensures survival in the same environment.
  • Sexual Reproduction: Involves two parents and mixes their genetic material. Each parent contributes special germ cells (gametes) that unite (fertilization) to develop into a new individual.

2. Asexual Reproduction Types

  • Fission: Division of an organism into two or more equal parts.
    - Binary fission: Splitting of a parent cell into two identical daughter cells (e.g., bacteria, protozoa).
    - Multiple fission: Nucleus divides repeatedly to produce many daughter cells (e.g., malarial parasites).
  • Fragmentation: Parent breaks into parts that regenerate into whole new individuals (e.g., fungi, worms, Spirogyra).
  • Budding: Outgrowth (bud) forms on the parent and pinches off (e.g., yeast, Hydra).
  • Sporulation: Formation of spores (reproductive cells) (e.g., most fungi).
  • Vegetative Propagation: Roots, stems, buds, or leaves give rise to new plants.
    - Natural: Stolons (Strawberry), Rhizome (Couch grass), Corm (Gladiolus), Tubers (Potato), Bulb (Lilies/onion).
    - Artificial: Grafting (Scion + Stock), Cutting (Hibiscus), Layering.
  • Parthenogenesis: Reproduction from an ovum without fertilization (e.g., honeybees: unfertilized egg develops into a drone/male).

3. Sexual Reproduction & Puberty

Involves fusion of gametes (sperm and egg) made via meiosis. Fertilization results in a zygote.

  • Primary sexual characteristics: Present at birth. Males (Penis, Scrotum, Testis). Females (Vagina, Uterus, Ovaries).
  • Secondary sexual characteristics: Develop during puberty.
    - Males: Body hair, muscle mass, deep voice, long bones.
    - Females: Breasts, wider hips, body hair, menstrual cycle begins.
  • Hormones: FSH and LH (secreted by pituitary gland) control ovaries and testes. LH triggers ovulation. FSH stimulates estrogen/progesterone (females) and testosterone (males). Testosterone drives male traits; Estrogen drives female traits.

4. Male Reproductive System

  • Testes: In the scrotum (lower temp). Site of sperm production in seminiferous tubules. Produce testosterone.
  • Epididymis: Sperm maturation.
  • Vas deferens: Conveys mature sperm to urethra.
  • Accessory Glands:
    - Seminal vesicles: Secrete fructose-rich fluid (energy).
    - Prostate gland: Alkaline secretions to counteract vagina acidity.
    - Bulbourethral glands: Lubricating mucus.
  • Sperm: Head (nucleus + enzyme), Midpiece (mitochondria), Tail/Flagellum (swimming). Semen is a mix of sperm and fluids.

5. Female Reproductive System

  • Ovaries: Produce/release eggs. Secrete estrogen and progesterone.
  • Oviduct (Uterine tube): Connects ovary to uterus. Lined with cilia to propel egg.
  • Uterus (womb): Hollow, pear-shaped. Inner lining (endometrium) houses embryo. Bottom is cervix.
  • Genitals (Vulva): Labia majora, Labia minora, Clitoris, Hymen.
  • Ovarian Cycle (28 days): Follicular Phase (Days 1-14), Ovulation (Day 14), Luteal Phase (Days 15-28). Corpus luteum forms after ovulation.
  • Menstrual Cycle: Flow Phase (Menstruation), Follicular Phase, Ovulation, Luteal Phase.
  • Menopause: End of menstrual cycles (ages 45-50). Decline in ovarian function.

6. Fertilization, Pregnancy & Twins

  • Fertilization: Fusion of sperm and egg nucleus, occurs in the oviduct.
  • Implantation: Embryo sinks into uterine lining.
  • Twins: Identical (one egg splits, same sex) vs. Fraternal (two eggs, two sperms, non-identical).
  • Development: Embryo -> Fetus (after 8 weeks). Amnion (fluid sac protects), Placenta (nutrient/gas exchange, connected by umbilical cord).

7. Birth Control (Contraception)

  • Natural: Abstinence (100% effective), Fertility awareness, Withdrawal, Body temperature, Cervical mucus.
  • Artificial: Female/Male condoms, Diaphragm, Spermicides, IUD, IUS, Contraceptive pill, Implant, Injection, Male sterilization (Vasectomy), Female sterilization (Laparotomy/Tubal ligation).

8. Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs)

  • Trichomoniasis: Parasite. Yellowish discharge, itching.
  • Chlamydia: Bacteria. Often undetected, painful urination, infertility.
  • Gonorrhea: Bacteria. Yellow pus, painful urination, can damage joints/heart.
  • Syphilis: Bacteria. Skin chances, can damage liver/brain.
  • HIV/AIDS: Virus. Kills immune cells. Transmitted via blood/fluids. Symptoms appear late. Prevention: Abstinence, no shared needles, condoms.

9. Glossary of Terms

Abstinence: Not engaging in sexual activity.
Amnion: Membrane enclosing the embryo.
Binary fission: Parent divides into two identical cells.
Blastocyst: Early embryonic structure.
Budding: New organism from an outgrowth.
Corpus luteum: Hormone-secreting structure after ovulation.
Endometrium: Lining of the uterus.
Epididymis: Where sperm matures.
Fertilization: Sperm and egg unite.
Fetus: Developing human from 9th week to birth.
Gamete: Mature sex cell.
Implantation: Blastocyst penetrates endometrium.
Menopause: Cessation of menstruation.
Placenta: Organ for nutrient exchange.
Puberty: When secondary sex characteristics develop.
Zygote: Cell from fusion of sperm and egg.

1. Seensa Walhormaataa (Reproduction)

Walhormaanni jechuun adeemsa maatiin sanyii (daa'imma) uumuu dha, kunis gosa lubbuu itti fufsiisa.

  • Walhormaata Saala Malee (Asexual): Sanyiin maatii tokko qofa irraa uumama. Qaama ykn seelii saalaa addaa hin barbaadu. Sanyii wal fakkaatu uuma. Faayidaa: seelii saalaa lama hin barbaadu, amala gaarii dhaala, naannoo tokkotti jiraachuuf gargaara.
  • Walhormaata Saalaan (Sexual): Maatii lama of keessatti qabata, jeneetiksii isaanii walitti makuun. Maatiin tokkoon tokkoon isaanii seelii saalaa (gametes) kennuun (fertilization) gara nama haaraatti guddatu.

2. Gosa Walhormaata Saala Malee

  • Fission (Addaan ba'uu): Organiizimii tokko kutaa lama ykn isaa ol ta'een adda ba'uu.
    - Binary fission: Seeliin haadhaa seelii intalaa lama wal fakkaatanitti adda ba'uu (fkn: baakteeriyaa, protozoa).
    - Multiple fission: Niwkiliyasiin irra deddeebi'ee adda ba'ee seelii intalaa hedduu uuma (fkn: paraasayitii malariaa).
  • Fragmentation (Caccabuu): Maatiin adda ba'ee, kutaan tokkoon tokkoon isaa organiizimii haaraa uuma (fkn: faanjii, raammoo, Spirogyra).
  • Budding (Dammaquu): Qaama maatii irraa wanti damee fakkaatu (bud) uumame adda ba'ee nama haaraa uuma (fkn: raayistii, Hydra).
  • Sporulation (Spoorii uumuu): Uumama seelii walhormaataa spoorii jedhamuun (fkn: faanjii hedduu).
  • Vegetative Propagation (Facaasuu biqiltuu): Hidda, jirma, damee ykn baala irraa biqiltuu haaraa uumama.
    - Uumamaa (Natural): Stolons (Strawberry), Rhizome (Couch grass), Corm (Gladiolus), Tubers (Dinnicha), Bulb (Qullubbii).
    - Nam-tolchee (Artificial): Grafting (Scion + Stock walitti qabuu), Cutting (Hibiscus), Layering (Jirma lafa jala galchuu).
  • Parthenogenesis: Ova (egg) fertilize hin taane irraa organiizimii uumuu (fkn: kanniisa, kuduraan malee (drone) uumama).

3. Walhormaata Saalaan fi Puberty (Guddina)

Seelii saalaa (spermii fi eggii) meiosis keessatti uumaman walitti makamuu of keessatti qabata. Fertilizationiin zygote uuma.

  • Amaloota saalaa jalqabaa (Primary): Dhalootaan qabaachuu. Dhiira (Qaama saalaa, Scrotum, Testis). Dubartii (Vagina, Uterus, Ovarii).
  • Amaloota saalaa lammaffaa (Secondary): Yeroo guddina (puberty) keessatti guddatu.
    - Dhiira: Rifeensa qaamaa, maashii, sagalee gadi aanaa, lafee dheeraa.
    - Dubartii: Harma, qeensa bal'aa, rifeensa qaamaa, mar'ummaan jalqaba.
  • Hormoonii: FSH fi LH (pituitary gland irraa) ovarii fi testis to'atu. LH ovulation kakaasa. FSH estroojinii/progesterone (dubartii) fi testosterone (dhiira) kakaasa. Testosterone amala dhiiraa, Estroojinii amala dubartii guddisa.

4. Sirna Walhormaata Dhiiraa

  • Testes: Scrotum keessa (ho'ina gadi aanaa). Bakka spermii oomishamu (seminiferous tubules). Testosterone oomisha.
  • Epididymis: Bakka spermii bilchina argatu.
  • Vas deferens: Spermii bilchaate gara urethra geessu.
  • Qaama Dabalataa (Accessory Glands):
    - Seminal vesicles: Dhangala'aa fructose qabu (humna) baasu.
    - Prostate gland: Dhangala'aa alkaline baasee asiidii vaginaa qabatu.
    - Bulbourethral glands: Dhangala'aa lubrication ta'u baasu.
  • Spermii: Mataa (nucleus + enzyme), Midpiece (mitochondria), eegee (swimming). Semen jechuun spermii fi dhangala'aa qaama dabalataa waliin makameedha.

5. Sirna Walhormaata Dubartii

  • Ovarii: Eggii oomishu fi gadhiisu. Estroojinii fi progesterone oomisha.
  • Oviduct (Fallopian tube): Ovarii gara uterusitti hidhu. Cilia qabaachuun eggii dhiibu.
  • Uterus (Gadameessa): Qaama duwwaa, boca pear. Keessaan (endometrium) embryo qabata. Gadiin isaa cervix jedhama.
  • Qaama saalaa alaa (Vulva): Labia majora, Labia minora, Clitoris, Hymen.
  • Ovarian Cycle (Guyyaa 28): Follicular Phase (Guyyaa 1-14), Ovulation (Guyyaa 14), Luteal Phase (Guyyaa 15-28). Erga ovulation booda corpus luteum uumama.
  • Menstrual Cycle: Flow Phase (Mar'ummaa), Follicular Phase, Ovulation, Luteal Phase.
  • Menopause: Xumura mar'ummaa (waggaa 45-50). Hojii ovarii hir'achuu.

6. Fertilization, Ulfaa fi Lakkuu (Twins)

  • Fertilization: Spermii fi eggii walitti makamuu, oviduct keessatti raawwatama.
  • Implantation: Embryo keessatti endometrium keessa qubatu.
  • Lakkuu (Twins): Identical (eggii tokko adda ba'ee, saala tokko) vs. Fraternal (eggii lama, spermii lama, wal hin fakkaatan).
  • Guddina: Embryo -> Fetus (torban 8 booda). Amnion (dhangala'aan qaama eegu), Placenta (nyaata fi gaasii jijjiiru, umbilical cordiin hidhama).

7. To'annoo Da'umsaa (Birth Control)

  • Uumamaa: Abstinence (100% gargaara), Fertility awareness, Withdrawal (cal'isuu), Ho'ina qaamaa, Cervical mucus.
  • Nam-tolchee: Condom dubartii/dhiiraa, Diaphragm, Spermicides, IUD, IUS, Qoricha kontraseptivii, Implant, Injection, Vasectomy (dhiira), Laparotomy/Tubal ligation (dubartii).

8. Dhukkuboota Saalaan Daddarban (STIs)

  • Trichomoniasis: Paraasayitii. Dhangala'aa keelloo, iitita.
  • Chlamydia: Baakteeriyaa. Yeroo baay'ee hin beekamu, fincila urna, maseena (infertility).
  • Gonorrhea: Baakteeriyaa. Dhangala'aa keelloo (pus), fincila urna, lafee fi onnee miidhuu danda'a.
  • Syphilis: Baakteeriyaa. Madaa gogaa, tiruu fi sammuu miidhuu danda'a.
  • HIV/AIDS: Vaayirasii. Seelii nageenya qaamaa ajjeesa. Dhiigaan/dhangala'aan daddarba. Mallattoon yeroo dheeraa booda mul'ata. Ittisuu: Abstinence, needilii waliin hin fayyadamin, condom.
