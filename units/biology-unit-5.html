1. Food and Nutrition

Food: Any beneficial substance eaten, drunk, or taken into the body to sustain life, provide energy, and promote growth.

  • Importance: Growth, Energy, Replacement of damaged tissues, Protection from diseases.
  • Nutrition: The process of taking in food and converting it into energy and nutrients (Ingestion, Digestion, Absorption, Assimilation).

2. Nutrients

Macronutrients: Needed in large amounts (Carbohydrates, Proteins, Fats).
Micronutrients: Needed in small amounts (Vitamins, Minerals).

  • Carbohydrates: Main energy source (1g = 16KJ). Types: Monosaccharides (Glucose, Fructose, Galactose), Disaccharides (Maltose, Sucrose, Lactose), Polysaccharides (Cellulose, Starch, Glycogen). Fiber prevents constipation.
  • Lipids (Fats & Oils): High energy (1g = 37KJ). Store energy, insulate, absorb fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K). Fats are solid (animals), Oils are liquid (plants).
  • Proteins: Made of amino acids. Contain Carbon, Hydrogen, Oxygen, Nitrogen. For growth, repair, and structure.
  • Vitamins: Organic molecules for metabolic regulation.
    - Water-soluble: B-complex, C.
    - Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K.
  • Minerals: Inorganic substances. Calcium (bones), Iron (hemoglobin), Iodine (thyroid), Phosphorus (bones/teeth).
  • Water: 60% of adult body. Solvent, transport, temperature regulation.

3. Balanced Diet & Malnutrition

  • Balanced Diet: Contains all nutrients in correct amounts. Depends on age, sex, activity.
  • Deficiency Diseases:
    - Marasmus: Inadequate carbohydrate/protein (thin, old-looking).
    - Kwashiorkor: Inadequate protein (swollen abdomen/legs, flaky skin).
    - Anemia: Lack of iron (weak, tired).
    - Rickets: Lack of Vitamin D (deformed bones).
    - Scurvy: Lack of Vitamin C (bleeding gums).
  • Malnutrition: Insufficient, excessive, or imbalanced nutrients.
    - Wasting: Low weight-for-height.
    - Stunting: Low height-for-age.
    - Underweight: Low weight-for-age (BMI < 18.5).
    - Overweight/Obesity: BMI 25-30 / >30. Risk of heart disease, diabetes.

4. Substance Abuse

Using drugs in the wrong way or excess amount.

  • Cigarettes: Contains Tar (cancer), CO (reduces O2), Nicotine (addictive stimulant).
  • Alcohol: Depressant. Causes loss of coordination, liver cirrhosis, brain damage.
  • Heroin: Synthetic morphine derivative. Powerful depressant, highly addictive.
  • Cannabis (Marijuana): Contains THC. Causes hallucinations, panic, psychosis in high amounts.
  • Khat (Catha edulis): Contains cathinone (stimulant). Causes depression when not used, socio-economic issues.
  • Doping: Using prohibited substances in sports (banned by WADA). Risks: heart disease, infertility, psychosis.

5. Infectious and Non-Infectious Diseases

  • Infectious Diseases: Caused by pathogens (bacteria, virus, protozoa, fungi). Transmitted via contact, water, insects.
    - HIV/AIDS: Attacks CD4 (T cells). Transmitted via blood/fluids. No cure, but ART controls it.
    - Tuberculosis (TB): Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Affects lungs. Droplet transmission. BCG vaccine.
    - Malaria: Plasmodium spp. Vector: Anopheles mosquito.
    - COVID-19: SARS-CoV-2. Respiratory droplets.
  • Non-Infectious Diseases: Not caused by pathogens.
    - Malnutrition (Scurvy, Kwashiorkor).
    - Cigarette smoke (Lung cancer, Bronchitis).
    - Genetic factors (Sickle cell anemia, Hemophilia).
    - Unhealthy diets (Obesity, High BP).
    - Old age (Osteoporosis, Arthritis, Alzheimer's).

1. Nyaata fi Sirna Nyaataa (Food and Nutrition)

Nyaata (Food): Wantoota faayidaa qaban kanneen lubbuu itti fufsiisuuf, humna kennuuf fi guddinaaf qaama keessa galfaman.

  • Faayidaa: Guddina, Humna, Seelii miidhame bakka buusuu, Dhukkuboota irraa eeguu.
  • Sirna Nyaataa (Nutrition): Adeemsa nyaata fudhachuu fi gara humnaatti jijjiiruu (Liqimsuu, Daakuu, Xuuxuu, Gara seelii geessuu).

2. Soorata (Nutrients)

Macronutrients: Baay'inaan barbaachisan (Karbohayidreeitii, Pirootiinii, Cooma).
Micronutrients: Muraasa barbaachisan (Vitamins, Minerals).

  • Karbohayidreeitii: Madda humna ijoo (1g = 16KJ). Gosa isaa: Monosaccharides (Glucose, Fructose, Galactose), Disaccharides (Maltose, Sucrose, Lactose), Polysaccharides (Cellulose, Starch, Glycogen). Fiber (roughage) garaa cimuu dhoorka.
  • Liipiidii (Cooma fi Zayitii): Humna olkaa'aa (1g = 37KJ). Humna kuusa, qaama ho'isa, vitamins A, D, E, K xuuxuuf gargaara. Coomni jabaa (bineensa), zayitiin dhangala'aa (biqiltuu).
  • Pirootiinii: Amino acids irraa ijaarama. Carbon, Hydrogen, Oxygen, Nitrogen qaba. Guddina, suphaa fi caasaa seelii.
  • Vitamins: Molekiyuloota orgaanikii sochii metaboolizimii to'atan.
    - Bishaan keessa bulbulu: B-complex, C.
    - Cooma keessa bulbulu: A, D, E, K.
  • Minerals: Wantoota orgaanikii hin taane. Calcium (lafee), Iron (dhiiga), Iodine (thyroid), Phosphorus (lafee/ilkaan).
  • Bishaan: Qaama nama guddaa 60% ta'a. Wantoota bulbulu, geejjibu, ho'ina qaamaa to'ata.

3. Sirna Nyaata Madaalawaa fi Hanga Nyaataa (Balanced Diet & Malnutrition)

  • Sirna Nyaata Madaalawaa: Soorata hunda baay'ina sirriin qabata. Umrii, saala, sochii irratti hundaa'a.
  • Dhukkuboota Hanqina Soorataa (Deficiency Diseases):
    - Marasmus: Hanqina karbohayidreeitii/pirootiinii (huqqisaa, fuulli dulloomaa).
    - Kwashiorkor: Hanqina pirootiinii (garaan fi miilli dhiita'a, gogaa gogaa).
    - Anemia: Hanqina sibiilaa (dadhabbii, tiruu).
    - Rickets: Hanqina Vitamin D (lafeen badaa'aa).
    - Scurvy: Hanqina Vitamin C (lafeen gumaa dhiigu).
  • Hanga Nyaataa (Malnutrition): Nyaata gahaa dhabuu, dabaluu ykn madaallii dhabuu.
    - Wasting: Ulfina dheerinaaf gadi aanaa.
    - Stunting: Dheerina umriif gadi aanaa.
    - Underweight: Ulfina umriif gadi aanaa (BMI < 18.5).
    - Overweight/Obesity: BMI 25-30 / >30. Dhukkuba onnee, sukkaara dhiitaa (diabetes) fiduu danda'a.

4. Fayyadama Qoricha Badaa (Substance Abuse)

Qoricha karaa dogoggora ykn baay'inaan fayyadamuu.

  • Sijaaraa: Tar (kanserii), CO (O2 hir'isa), Nicotine (addictive stimulant) qaba.
  • Alkoolii: Depressant. Madaallii dhabuu, tiruu (cirrhosis), sammuu miidha.
  • Heroin: Morphine irraa hojjetame. Depressant cimaa, addictive.
  • Cannabis (Marijuana): THC qaba. Baay'inaan yoo fayyadame hallucination, panic, psychosis fidu.
  • Jimaa (Khat): Cathinone (stimulant) qaba. Yoo dhaabne depression, rakkoo hawaasummaa fi dinagdee fidu.
  • Doping: Ispoortii keessatti wantoota dhorkaman fayyadamuu (WADA dhoorka). Balaa: dhukkuba onnee, maseena, psychosis.

5. Dhukkuboota Daddarbanii fi Hin Daddarbne (Infectious & Non-Infectious)

  • Dhukkuboota Daddarban (Infectious): Paatojeenoota (baakteeriyaa, vaayirasii, protozoa, faanjii) irraa dhufu. Quunnamtii, bishaan, ilbiisaan daddarbu.
    - HIV/AIDS: Seelii nageenyaa (CD4/T cells) miidha. Dhiigaan/dhangala'aan daddarba. Qorichi hin jiru, garuu ART waliin jiraachuu danda'a.
    - Tuberculosis (TB): Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Somba miidha. Haaraan daddarba. Talaallii BCG.
    - Malaria: Plasmodium spp. Vector: Bookee Anopheles.
    - COVID-19: SARS-CoV-2. Haaraan daddarba.
  • Dhukkuboota Hin Daddarbne (Non-Infectious): Paatojeenootaan hin dhufan.
    - Hanga nyaataa (Scurvy, Kwashiorkor).
    - Aara sijaaraa (Kanserii sombaa, Bronchitis).
    - Jeneetiksii (Sickle cell anemia, Hemophilia).
    - Nyaata gadhee (Obesity, High BP).
    - Umurii dulluma (Osteoporosis, Arthritis, Alzheimer's).
