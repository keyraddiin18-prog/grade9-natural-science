1. Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, students will be able to:

  • Define food, nutrition, and nutrients.
  • Classify nutrients into macronutrients and micronutrients.
  • Identify the sources and functions of various vitamins and minerals.
  • Describe the causes and symptoms of deficiency diseases and malnutrition.
  • Explain the health risks associated with substance abuse.
  • Differentiate between infectious and non-infectious diseases.

2. Introduction to Food and Nutrition

Food is any beneficial substance eaten or drunk to sustain life, provide energy, and promote growth. Nutrition is the process of taking in food and converting it into energy and nutrients (Ingestion, Digestion, Absorption, Assimilation).

3. Detailed Notes

Part 1: Nutrients

  • Macronutrients: Needed in large amounts (Carbohydrates, Proteins, Lipids).
    - Carbohydrates: Main energy source (1g = 16KJ). Types: Monosaccharides (Glucose), Disaccharides (Sucrose), Polysaccharides (Starch). Fiber prevents constipation.
    - Lipids (Fats & Oils): High energy (1g = 37KJ). Fats are solid (animals), Oils are liquid (plants).
    - Proteins: Made of amino acids. Contain Nitrogen. For growth and repair.
  • Micronutrients: Needed in small amounts (Vitamins, Minerals).
    - Vitamins: Water-soluble (B, C) and Fat-soluble (A, D, E, K).
    - Minerals: Calcium (bones), Iron (hemoglobin), Iodine (thyroid), Phosphorus (bones/teeth).
  • Water: 60% of adult body. Solvent and transport medium.

Part 2: Balanced Diet & Malnutrition

  • Balanced Diet: Contains all nutrients in correct amounts.
  • Deficiency Diseases: Marasmus (lack of carbs/protein), Kwashiorkor (lack of protein), Anemia (lack of iron), Rickets (lack of Vit D), Scurvy (lack of Vit C).
  • Malnutrition: Insufficient, excessive, or imbalanced nutrients.
    - Wasting: Low weight-for-height.
    - Stunting: Low height-for-age.
    - Underweight: Low weight-for-age (BMI < 18.5).
    - Overweight/Obesity: BMI 25-30 / >30.

Part 3: Substance Abuse

  • Cigarettes: Tar (cancer), CO (reduces O2), Nicotine (addictive).
  • Alcohol: Depressant. Causes liver cirrhosis, brain damage.
  • Heroin: Powerful depressant, highly addictive.
  • Cannabis: Contains THC. Causes hallucinations in high amounts.
  • Khat (Catha edulis): Contains cathinone (stimulant). Causes socio-economic and health issues.
  • Doping: Prohibited substances in sports (banned by WADA). Risks: heart disease, infertility, psychosis.

Part 4: Infectious and Non-Infectious Diseases

  • Infectious: Caused by pathogens (bacteria, virus, protozoa, fungi). Transmitted via contact, water, insects.
    - HIV/AIDS: Attacks CD4 (T cells). Transmitted via blood/fluids. ART controls it.
    - Tuberculosis (TB): Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Affects lungs. BCG vaccine.
    - Malaria: Plasmodium spp. Vector: Anopheles mosquito.
    - COVID-19: SARS-CoV-2. Respiratory droplets.
  • Non-Infectious: Not caused by pathogens.
    - Malnutrition (Scurvy), Cigarette smoke (Lung cancer), Genetic factors (Sickle cell anemia), Unhealthy diets (Obesity), Old age (Osteoporosis).

5. Summary

  • Food provides energy, growth, repair, and protection.
  • A balanced diet includes all macronutrients and micronutrients.
  • Malnutrition includes both undernutrition (stunting, wasting) and overnutrition (obesity).
  • Substance abuse (smoking, alcohol, drugs) has severe health consequences.
  • Diseases are classified as infectious (transmissible) or non-infectious (genetic, lifestyle, age).

1. Kaayyoo Barnootaa (Learning Objectives)

Erga kutaa kana xumurani booda, barattoonni:

  • Nyaata, sirna nyaataa fi soorata hiikanii ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Soorata gara macronutrients fi micronutrientsitti ramaduu ni danda'u.
  • Madda fi hojii vitamins fi minerals adda addaa adda baasuu ni danda'u.
  • Sababaa fi mallattoo dhukkuboota hanqina soorataa fi hanga nyaataa ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Balaa fayyaa fayyadama qoricha badaa waliin walqabatu ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Dhukkuboota daddarbanii fi hin daddarbne gidduu garaagarummaa adda baasuu ni danda'u.

2. Seensa Nyaata fi Sirna Nyaataa (Introduction)

Nyaanni wantoota faayidaa qaban kanneen lubbuu itti fufsiisuuf, humna kennuuf fi guddinaaf qaama keessa galfaman. Sirni Nyaataa adeemsa nyaata fudhachuu fi gara humnaatti jijjiiruu (Liqimsuu, Daakuu, Xuuxuu, Gara seelii geessuu).

3. Yaadannoo Barnootaa (Detailed Notes)

Kutaa 1: Soorata (Nutrients)

  • Macronutrients: Baay'inaan barbaachisan (Karbohayidreeitii, Pirootiinii, Cooma).
    - Karbohayidreeitii: Madda humna ijoo (1g = 16KJ). Gosa: Monosaccharides (Glucose), Disaccharides (Sucrose), Polysaccharides (Starch). Fiber garaa cimuu dhoorka.
    - Liipiidii (Cooma fi Zayitii): Humna olkaa'aa (1g = 37KJ). Coomni jabaa (bineensa), zayitiin dhangala'aa (biqiltuu).
    - Pirootiinii: Amino acids irraa ijaarama. Nitrogen qaba. Guddina fi suphaa seelii.
  • Micronutrients: Muraasa barbaachisan (Vitamins, Minerals).
    - Vitamins: Bishaan keessa bulbulu (B, C) fi Cooma keessa bulbulu (A, D, E, K).
    - Minerals: Calcium (lafee), Iron (dhiiga), Iodine (thyroid), Phosphorus (lafee/ilkaan).
  • Bishaan: Qaama nama guddaa 60% ta'a. Wantoota bulbulu fi geejjibu.

Kutaa 2: Sirna Nyaata Madaalawaa fi Hanga Nyaataa

  • Sirna Nyaata Madaalawaa: Soorata hunda baay'ina sirriin qabata.
  • Dhukkuboota Hanqina Soorataa: Marasmus (hanqina carb/protein), Kwashiorkor (hanqina protein), Anemia (hanqina iron), Rickets (hanqina Vit D), Scurvy (hanqina Vit C).
  • Hanga Nyaataa (Malnutrition): Nyaata gahaa dhabuu, dabaluu ykn madaallii dhabuu.
    - Wasting: Ulfina dheerinaaf gadi aanaa.
    - Stunting: Dheerina umriif gadi aanaa.
    - Underweight: Ulfina umriif gadi aanaa (BMI < 18.5).
    - Overweight/Obesity: BMI 25-30 / >30.

Kutaa 3: Fayyadama Qoricha Badaa (Substance Abuse)

  • Sijaaraa: Tar (kanserii), CO (O2 hir'isa), Nicotine (addictive).
  • Alkoolii: Depressant. Tiruu (cirrhosis), sammuu miidha.
  • Heroin: Depressant cimaa, addictive.
  • Cannabis: THC qaba. Baay'inaan yoo fayyadame hallucination fidu.
  • Jimaa (Khat): Cathinone (stimulant) qaba. Rakkoo hawaasummaa fi dinagdee fidu.
  • Doping: Ispoortii keessatti wantoota dhorkaman fayyadamuu (WADA dhoorka). Balaa: dhukkuba onnee, maseena, psychosis.

Kutaa 4: Dhukkuboota Daddarbanii fi Hin Daddarbne

  • Dhukkuboota Daddarban (Infectious): Paatojeenoota (baakteeriyaa, vaayirasii, protozoa, faanjii) irraa dhufu.
    - HIV/AIDS: Seelii nageenyaa (CD4/T cells) miidha. ART waliin jiraachuu danda'a.
    - Tuberculosis (TB): Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Somba miidha. Talaallii BCG.
    - Malaria: Plasmodium spp. Vector: Bookee Anopheles.
    - COVID-19: SARS-CoV-2. Haaraan daddarba.
  • Dhukkuboota Hin Daddarbne (Non-Infectious): Paatojeenootaan hin dhufan.
    - Hanga nyaataa (Scurvy), Aara sijaaraa (Kanserii sombaa), Jeneetiksii (Sickle cell anemia), Nyaata gadhee (Obesity), Umurii dulluma (Osteoporosis).

5. Cuunfaa (Summary)

  • Nyaanni humna, guddina, suphaa fi eegumsa kennu.
  • Sirni nyaata madaalawaa macronutrients fi micronutrients hunda of keessatti qabata.
  • Hangi nyaataa hanqina (stunting, wasting) fi dabalata (obesity) of keessatti qabata.
  • Fayyadamni qoricha badaa (sijaaraa, alkoolii, qoricha) balaa fayyaa cimaa qaba.
  • Dhukkuboonni daddarbanii fi hin daddarbne jedhamanii ramadamu.
