1. Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, students will be able to:

  • Define ecology and explain the levels of ecological organization.
  • Differentiate between biotic and abiotic factors.
  • Describe the characteristics of major terrestrial and aquatic biomes.
  • Explain the process of ecological succession (Primary and Secondary).
  • Identify different ecological relationships (Competition, Predation, Mutualism, etc.).

2. Introduction to Ecology

Ecology is the branch of biology that studies the interactions between organisms and their environment. It comes from the Greek words oikos (home) and logos (study).

3. Detailed Notes

Part 1: Key Terms & Components

  • Ecosystem: All organisms in a community plus all non-living factors they interact with.
  • Producers: Make their own food using sunlight (e.g., plants).
  • Consumers: Eat other organisms (e.g., animals).
  • Decomposers: Break down dead material (e.g., bacteria, fungi).
  • Habitat: The place where an organism lives.
  • Niche: The role an organism performs in its habitat.
  • Abiotic Factors: Non-living factors (Sunlight, Temperature, Water, Nutrients, Oxygen, Salinity, Wind).
  • Biotic Factors: Living factors (Predators, Herbivores, Pollinators, Parasites, Competitors).

Part 2: Ecological Levels

Organism → Population (same species, same area) → Community (different species interacting) → Ecosystem (community + abiotic factors) → Biosphere (global ecosystem).

Part 3: Biomes

  • Tropical Rain Forest: High rainfall, high biodiversity, broad-leaf evergreen trees.
  • Desert: Less than 10cm rain/year, extreme temperatures, cacti, nocturnal animals.
  • Savanna: Warm year-round, scattered trees, fire-adapted, zebras/lions.
  • Temperate Grassland: Hot summers, cold winters, deep grass roots.
  • Boreal Forest (Taiga): Snow precipitation, cold winters, coniferous trees, acidic soil.
  • Temperate Broad-Leaf Forest: Deciduous trees (shed leaves).
  • Tundra: Cold, permafrost (frozen soil), mosses, lichens, dwarf shrubs.
  • Freshwater: Lakes, Ponds, Rivers/Streams, Wetlands.
  • Marine: Oceans, Coral Reefs (most diverse), Estuaries (transition between river and sea).

Part 4: Ecological Succession

  • Primary Succession: Occurs where no life existed (e.g., new volcanic island). Starts with prokaryotes, lichens, and mosses. Takes thousands of years.
  • Secondary Succession: Occurs where a disturbance removed species but left soil (e.g., abandoned farm). Faster.
  • Key Concepts: Climax Community (final stable stage), Facilitation (early species help later ones), Inhibition (early species hinder later ones), Tolerance (species thrive in conditions created by early ones).

Part 5: Ecological Relationships

  • Competition (-/-): Both harmed by limited resources.
  • Predation (+/-): Predator kills and eats prey.
  • Herbivory (+/-): Herbivore eats parts of a plant, causing harm but usually not death.
  • Parasitism (+/-): Parasite derives nourishment from a host, harming it.
  • Mutualism (+/+): Both species benefit (e.g., termites and gut microbes).
  • Commensalism (+/0): One benefits, the other is unaffected.

4. Key Terms

5. Summary

  • Ecology studies the interactions between living (biotic) and non-living (abiotic) things.
  • Life is organized from organisms to populations, communities, ecosystems, and the biosphere.
  • Biomes are large ecosystems (e.g., Tundra, Desert, Tropical Rainforest) determined by climate.
  • Ecological succession is the process of change in species composition over time.
  • Organisms interact in various ways: competition, predation, mutualism, etc.

1. Kaayyoo Barnootaa (Learning Objectives)

Erga kutaa kana xumurani booda, barattoonni:

  • Ekoolojii hiikanii fi sadarkaalee qindoomina ekoolojii ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Wantoota lubbuu qaban (biotic) fi hin qabne (abiotic) gidduu garaagarummaa adda baasuu ni danda'u.
  • Amaloota biyoomota lafa irraa fi bishaan keessaa ijoo ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Adeemsa itti fufiinsa ekoolojii (Jalqabaa fi Lammaffaa) ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Hariiroo ekoolojii adda addaa (Dorgommii, Addaan nyaachuu, Mutualism, fi kkf) adda baasuu ni danda'u.

2. Seensa Ekoolojii (Introduction)

Ekoolojiin damee saayinsii lubbuu kan hariiroo organiizimoota fi naannoo isaanii qoratu dha. Jecha Giriikii oikos (mana) fi logos (qorannoo) irraa dhufe.

3. Yaadannoo Barnootaa (Detailed Notes)

Kutaa 1: Jechoota Ijoo fi Qindoomina

  • Ekosistemii: Organiizimoota hawaasa keessa jiran hunda fi wantoota lubbuu hin qabne isaan waliin hojjetan.
  • Oomishitoota (Producers): Humna aduu fayyadamuun nyaata ofii isaa oomishan (fkn: biqiltuu).
  • Fayyadamtoota (Consumers): Organiizimii biraa nyaatan (fkn: bineensota).
  • Balleessitoota (Decomposers): Wantoota du'an daakanii soorata deebisan (fkn: baakteeriyaa, faanjii).
  • Bakka Jireenyaa (Habitat): Bakka organiizimii tokko itti jiraatu.
  • Gahee (Niche): Hojii organiizimii tokko bakka jireenya isaatti raawwatu.
  • Wantoota Lubbuu Hin Qabne (Abiotic): Ifa aduu, Ho'ina, Bishaan, Soorata, Oksijiinii, Soogidda, Bubbee.
  • Wantoota Lubbuu Qaban (Biotic): Addaan nyaattoota, Nyaattota biqiltuu, Pollinators, Paraasayitoota, Dorgomtoota.

Kutaa 2: Sadarkaalee Ekoolojii

Organiizimii → Baay'ina (Population) (gosa tokko, bakka tokko) → Hawaasa (Community) (gosa adda addaa waliin jiraatan) → Ekosistemii (hawaasa + wantoota lubbuu hin qabne) → Baayosfeerii (Biosphere) (ekosistemii addunyaa hunda).

Kutaa 3: Biyoomota (Biomes)

  • Bosona Roobaa Ho'aa (Tropical Rain Forest): Roobni baay'ee, lubbuun adda addaa, biqiltoonni baala bal'aa.
  • Gammoojjii (Desert): Roobni < 10cm, ho'inni cimaa, cacti, bineensonni halkan socho'an.
  • Savanna: Ho'ina wal irra hin deebine, muka muraasa, ibidda dandamatu, zebra fi leenca.
  • Temperate Grassland: Hiddi margaa dheeraa, bokkaa ittisuuf gargaara.
  • Boreal Forest (Taiga): Qarqara koolaa, roobni cabbii, biqiltoonni coniferous.
  • Temperate Broad-Leaf Forest: Biqiltoonni baala gadi lakkisan (deciduous).
  • Tundra: Qorra cimaa, permafrost (biyyoo yeroo hunda cabbii), mosses fi lichens.
  • Bishaan Curee: Haroo, Laga, Wetlands.
  • Bishaan Soogidda: Galaana, Coral Reefs, Estuaries (laga fi galaana walitti makamu).

Kutaa 4: Itti Fufiinsa Ekoolojii (Ecological Succession)

  • Primary Succession (Jalqabaa): Bakka lubbuun hin jiraanne (fkn: odola volcano). Prokaryotes, lichens fi mosses irraa jalqaba. Waggoota kumaatama fudhata.
  • Secondary Succession (Lammaffaa): Bakka balaa wantoota jiran balleesse garuu biyyoo hafe (fkn: qonna dhiifame). Saffisaan raawwata.
  • Yaadota Ijoo: Climax Community (sadarkaa dhumaa), Facilitation (gosa duraa kan boodaa gargaaru), Inhibition (gosa duraa kan boodaa dhorku), Tolerance (haala uumame keessa jiraachuu).

Kutaa 5: Hariiroo Ekoolojii (Ecological Relationships)

  • Dorgommii (Competition -/-): Lamaanuu soorata dhabuun miidhamu.
  • Addaan Nyaachuu (Predation +/-): Addaan nyaataan waan nyaatamu ajjeesee nyaata.
  • Nyaata Biqiltuu (Herbivory +/-): Bineensi biqiltuu nyaachuun miidha, garuu ajjeesuu hin danda'u.
  • Paraasitizimii (Parasitism +/-): Paraasayitiin qaama kaan irraa nyaata, miidha.
  • Mutualism (+/+): Lamaanuu faayidaa argatu (fkn: mycorrhizae).
  • Commensalism (+/0): Tokko faayidaa argata, inni kaan hin miidhamu.

4. Jechoota Ijoo (Key Terms)

JechaHiika

Itti Fufiinsa (Succession)Jijjiirama suuta ta'e gosa lubbuu naannoo tokko keessatti.

5. Cuunfaa (Summary)

  • Ekoolojiin hariiroo wantoota lubbuu qabanii fi hin qabne qorata.
  • Lubbuun sadarkaa organiizimii irraa hanga baayosfeeriitti qindaa'a.
  • Biyoomoonni ekosistemoota gurguddoo qilleensaatiin adda ba'an (fkn: Tundra, Gammoojjii, Bosona Roobaa).
  • Itti fufiinsi ekoolojii adeemsa jijjiirama gosa lubbuu yeroo waliinii dha.
  • Organiizimoonni karaa adda addaatiin hariiroo qabu: dorgommii, addaan nyaachuu, mutualism, fi kkf.
