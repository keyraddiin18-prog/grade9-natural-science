1. Definitions of Common Ecological Terms

  • Ecology: The branch of biology that studies interactions between organisms and their environment (from Greek oikos = home, logos = study).
  • Ecosystem: All organisms in a community plus all non-living factors they interact with. A self-supporting system.
  • Producers: Organisms that produce their own food using sunlight (e.g., plants, algae).
  • Consumers: Organisms that cannot make their own food and eat other organisms (e.g., animals).
  • Decomposers: Organisms that break down dead material and recycle nutrients (e.g., bacteria, fungi).
  • Habitat: The place where an organism lives.
  • Niche: The role or job an organism performs in its habitat.

2. Biotic and Abiotic Components

  • Abiotic Factors: Non-living components (Sunlight, Temperature, Water, Nutrients, Oxygen, Salinity, Wind, Natural Disturbances).
  • Biotic Factors: Living components affecting species distribution (Predators, Herbivores, Pollinators, Parasites, Pathogens, Competitors).

3. Ecological Levels of Organization

Organism → Population (same species, same area) → Community (different species interacting) → Ecosystem (community + abiotic factors) → Biosphere (global ecosystem).

4. Ecosystems

Consists of biotic and abiotic components. Functioning includes Energy Flow (Sun), Nutrient Cycling (Decomposers), and Food Chains/Webs. Types:

  • Terrestrial: Found on land (Forests, Grasslands, Deserts).
  • Aquatic: Found in water (Freshwater: lakes, ponds, rivers; Marine: oceans, coastal).

5. Biomes

A major life zone characterized by climate and vegetation.

  • Tropical Rain Forest: Equatorial, high rainfall, high biodiversity, broad-leaf evergreen trees, intense competition for light.
  • Desert: 30° N/S latitude, less than 10cm rain/year, extreme temperatures, cacti, nocturnal animals.
  • Savanna: Equatorial/sub-equatorial, 30-50cm rain, warm year-round, scattered trees, fire-adapted, zebras/lions.
  • Temperate Grassland: North America, 25-100cm rain, hot summers, cold winters, deep grass roots.
  • Boreal Forest (Taiga): Near poles, snow precipitation, cold winters, coniferous trees, acidic soil.
  • Temperate Broad-Leaf Forest: Mid-latitudes, 70-200cm rain, deciduous trees (shed leaves).
  • Tundra: Covers 20% of land, cold, permafrost (frozen soil), mosses, lichens, dwarf shrubs.
  • Freshwater: Lakes (standing water), Ponds (shallow), Rivers/Streams (moving, highly oxygenated), Wetlands (saturated soil).
  • Marine: Oceans (Pelagic zone, Abyssal zone), Coral Reefs (most diverse), Estuaries (transition between river and sea).

6. Ecological Succession

  • Primary Succession: Occurs where no life existed (e.g., new volcanic island). Starts with prokaryotes, lichens, and mosses. Takes thousands of years.
  • Secondary Succession: Occurs where a disturbance removed species but left soil (e.g., abandoned farm). Faster. Starts with herbaceous species, then shrubs, then trees.
  • Key Concepts: Climax Community (final stable stage), Facilitation (early species help later ones), Inhibition (early species hinder later ones), Tolerance (species thrive in conditions created by early ones).

7. Ecological Relationships

  • Competition (-/-): Both harmed by limited resources (e.g., weeds vs. garden plants).
  • Predation (+/-): Predator kills and eats prey (e.g., rotifer eating protist).
  • Herbivory (+/-): Herbivore eats parts of a plant, causing harm but usually not death (e.g., cattle eating grass).
  • Parasitism (+/-): Parasite derives nourishment from a host, harming it (e.g., ticks, roundworms).
  • Mutualism (+/+): Both species benefit (e.g., termites and gut microbes, mycorrhizae).
  • Commensalism (+/0): One benefits, the other is unaffected (e.g., cattle egrets eating flushed insects).

1. Hiika Jechoota Ekoolojii Ijoo

  • Ekoolojii (Ecology): Damee saayinsii lubbuu kan hariiroo organiizimoota fi naannoo isaanii qoratu (jecha Giriikii oikos = mana, logos = qorannoo irraa).
  • Ekosistemii (Ecosystem): Organiizimoota hawaasa tokko keessa jiran hunda fi wantoota lubbuu hin qabne isaan waliin hojjetan. Sirna of danda'e.
  • Oomishitoota (Producers): Organiizimoota humna aduu fayyadamuun nyaata ofii isaa oomishan (fkn: biqiltuu, algae).
  • Fayyadamtoota (Consumers): Organiizimoota nyaata ofii isaa oomishuu hin dandeenye, kanneen organiizimii biraa nyaatan (fkn: bineensota).
  • Balleessitoota (Decomposers): Organiizimoota wantoota du'an daakanii soorata deebisan (fkn: baakteeriyaa, faanjii).
  • Bakka Jireenyaa (Habitat): Bakka organiizimii tokko itti jiraatu.
  • Gahee (Niche): Hojii ykn gahee organiizimii tokko bakka jireenya isaa keessatti raawwatu.

2. Wantoota Lubbuu Qaban fi Hin Qabne (Biotic & Abiotic)

  • Wantoota Lubbuu Hin Qabne (Abiotic Factors): Ifa aduu, Ho'ina, Bishaan, Soorata, Oksijiinii, Soogidda (salinity), Bubbee, Balaawwa uumamaa.
  • Wantoota Lubbuu Qaban (Biotic Factors): Addaan nyaattoota, Nyaattota biqiltuu, Pollinators, Paraasayitoota, Paatojeenoota, Dorgomtoota.

3. Sadarkaalee Ekoolojii

Organiizimii → Baay'ina (Population) (gosa tokko, bakka tokko) → Hawaasa (Community) (gosa adda addaa waliin jiraatan) → Ekosistemii (hawaasa + wantoota lubbuu hin qabne) → Baayosfeerii (Biosphere) (ekosistemii addunyaa hunda).

4. Ekosistemoota

Wantoota lubbuu qaban fi hin qabne of keessatti qabata. Sochii isaa: Yaa'a humnaa (Aduu), Naanna'iinsa soorataa (Balleessitoota), Saffisa nyaataa. Gosa isaa:

  • Lafa Irraa (Terrestrial): Bosona, Marga, Gammoojjii.
  • Bishaan Keessaa (Aquatic): Bishaan curee (haroo, laga) fi Bishaan soogidda (galaana, qarqara galaanaa).

5. Biyoomota (Biomes)

Naannoo guddaa qilleensa fi biqiltootaan adda ba'u.

  • Tropical Rain Forest: Qarqara lafaa, roobni baay'ee, lubbuun adda addaa, biqiltoonni baala bal'aa, dorgommiin ifaaf godhamu cimaa.
  • Gammoojjii (Desert): Roobni < 10cm, ho'inni cimaa, biqiltoonni cacti, bineensonni halkan socho'an.
  • Savanna: Roobni 30-50cm, ho'ina wal irra hin deebine, muka muraasa, ibidda dandamatu, zebra fi leenca.
  • Temperate Grassland: Roobni 25-100cm, hiddi margaa dheeraa, bokkaa ittisuuf gargaara.
  • Boreal Forest (Taiga): Qarqara koolaa, roobni cabbii, biqiltoonni coniferous, biyyoo asiidii.
  • Temperate Broad-Leaf Forest: Giddu-galeessa, roobni 70-200cm, biqiltoonni baala gadi lakkisan (deciduous).
  • Tundra: Lafa 20% qabata, qorra cimaa, permafrost (biyyoo yeroo hunda cabbii), mosses, lichens.
  • Bishaan Curee (Freshwater): Haroo (bishaan dhaabbataa), Laga (socho'aa, O2 baay'ee), Wetlands (biyyoo bishaan itti baay'ate).
  • Bishaan Soogidda (Marine): Galaana, Coral Reefs (baay'ee adda addaa), Estuaries (laga fi galaana walitti makamu).

6. Itti Fufiinsa Ekoolojii (Ecological Succession)

  • Primary Succession (Jalqabaa): Bakka lubbuun hin jiraanne (fkn: odola volcano). Prokaryotes, lichens fi mosses irraa jalqaba. Waggoota kumaatama fudhata.
  • Secondary Succession (Lammaffaa): Bakka balaa wantoota jiran balleesse garuu biyyoo hafe (fkn: qonna dhiifame). Saffisaan raawwata.
  • Yaadota Ijoo: Climax Community (sadarkaa dhumaa), Facilitation (gosa duraa kan boodaa gargaaru), Inhibition (gosa duraa kan boodaa dhorku), Tolerance (haala uumame keessa jiraachuu).

7. Hariiroo Ekoolojii

  • Dorgommii (Competition -/-): Lamaanuu soorata dhabuun miidhamu (fkn: aramaa fi biqiltuu).
  • Addaan Nyaachuu (Predation +/-): Addaan nyaataan waan nyaatamu ajjeesee nyaata (fkn: hawk fi hantuuta).
  • Nyaata Biqiltuu (Herbivory +/-): Bineensi biqiltuu nyaachuun miidha, garuu ajjeesuu hin danda'u (fkn: loon marga nyaachuu).
  • Paraasitizimii (Parasitism +/-): Paraasayitiin qaama kaan irraa nyaata, miidha (fkn: silmii fi raammoo).
  • Mutualism (+/+): Lamaanuu faayidaa argatu (fkn: mycorrhizae fi biqiltuu).
  • Commensalism (+/0): Tokko faayidaa argata, inni kaan hin miidhamu (fkn: simbirroon loon hordoftee ilbiisa nyaachuu).
