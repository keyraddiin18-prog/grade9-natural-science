1. Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, students will be able to:

  • Define a cell and state the three core tenets of Cell Theory.
  • Identify the structure and function of various cell organelles.
  • Differentiate between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells.
  • Compare and contrast plant and animal cells.
  • Explain the processes of passive and active transport (Diffusion, Osmosis).
  • Describe the levels of biological organization from atoms to the biosphere.

2. Introduction

The cell is the smallest and basic unit of living things. Organisms are classified as unicellular (e.g., bacteria) or multicellular (e.g., plants, humans). The discovery of cells was made possible by the invention of the microscope.

3. Detailed Notes

Part 1: Cell Theory

  • Robert Hooke (1665): Observed cork and coined the term "cell."
  • Antonie van Leeuwenhoek (1674): Observed living cells and called them "animalcules."
  • Matthias Schleiden (1838): Proposed all plants are made of cells.
  • Theodor Schwann (1839): Concluded all living organisms are composed of cells.
  • Rudolf Virchow (1856): Stated all cells arise from pre-existing cells ("Omnis cellula e cellula").

Core Tenets: 1) All living things are made of one or more cells. 2) The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. 3) All cells come from pre-existing cells.

Part 2: Cell Structure and Function (Organelles)

  • Cell Wall: Rigid structure for support. Found in plants, fungi, algae, bacteria. Absent in animals.
  • Cell Membrane: Selectively permeable outer covering controlling entry/exit.
  • Cytoplasm: Thick fluid (70-80% water) where chemical reactions occur.
  • Nucleus: Largest organelle. Contains chromosomes (DNA). Controls cell division. Has a Nucleolus (makes ribosomes).
  • Ribosomes: Synthesize proteins (free or attached to RER).
  • Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER):
    - Rough ER: Has ribosomes; synthesizes proteins.
    - Smooth ER: No ribosomes; synthesizes lipids.
  • Vesicles: Transport materials.
  • Golgi Apparatus: Modifies, packages, and distributes proteins/lipids.
  • Mitochondria: "Powerhouse of the cell." Produces ATP energy via respiration.
  • Lysosomes: Digestive enzymes that break down waste and bacteria.
  • Vacuoles: Storage. Plant cells have large central vacuoles; animal cells have small ones.
  • Chloroplasts: Site of photosynthesis (contains chlorophyll). Found in plants only.
  • Centrioles & Microtubules: Help in cell division. Centrioles are only in animal cells.
  • Cilia & Flagella: For locomotion (Cilia = short/many, Flagella = long/few).

Part 3: Types of Cells

  • Prokaryotic: "Before nucleus." Small (1-10 μm), no membrane-bound organelles, circular DNA, cell wall present (peptidoglycan). E.g., Bacteria.
  • Eukaryotic: "True nucleus." Larger (10-100 μm), has membrane-bound organelles, linear DNA. E.g., Plants, Animals.

Part 4: Plant vs. Animal Cells

  • Plant Cells: Rigid cell wall, chloroplasts, large permanent vacuole.
  • Animal Cells: Irregular shape, centrioles, lysosomes, no cell wall, small temporary vacuoles.

Part 5: Observing Cells & Transport

  • Staining: Iodine for onion cells, Methylene Blue for cheek cells.
  • Passive Transport: No energy needed.
    - Diffusion: High to low concentration.
    - Osmosis: Water moving across a membrane.
  • Active Transport: Movement from low to high concentration (against gradient) using energy (ATP).
  • Tonicity:
    - Hypertonic: Water leaves cell (cell shrinks/plasmolysis).
    - Isotonic: Equal water movement.
    - Hypotonic: Water enters cell (cell swells/turgid or bursts).

Part 6: Levels of Biological Organization

Atoms → Molecules → Organelles → Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ System → Organism → Population → Community → Ecosystem → Biosphere.

4. Key Terms

TermMeaning
CellThe basic structural and functional unit of life.
ProkaryoteCell without a true nucleus (Bacteria).
EukaryoteCell with a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
OrganellesSpecialized structures within a cell (e.g., Mitochondria).
DiffusionMovement of molecules from high to low concentration.
OsmosisMovement of water across a selectively permeable membrane.
Active TransportMovement of molecules against a gradient, requiring energy.
HomeostasisMaintaining a stable internal environment in the cell.

5. Summary

  • Cells are the basic units of life, governed by the principles of Cell Theory.
  • Eukaryotic cells contain specialized organelles (like mitochondria and nucleus) that perform specific functions.
  • Plant and animal cells share common features but differ in structures like the cell wall and chloroplasts.
  • Cells interact with their environment through diffusion, osmosis, and active transport.
  • Life is organized hierarchically from atoms to the biosphere.

1. Kaayyoo Barnootaa (Learning Objectives)

Erga kutaa kana xumurani booda, barattoonni:

  • Seelii hiikanii yaadota ijoo Yaada Seelii (Cell Theory) sadan ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Caasaa fi hojii organelles (qaamoota seelii) adda addaa adda baasuu ni danda'u.
  • Seelii prokaryotic fi eukaryotic gidduu garaagarummaa ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Seelii biqiltuu fi bineensaa wal bira qabanii ilaaluu ni danda'u.
  • Adeemsa passive fi active transport (Diffusion, Osmosis) ibsuu ni danda'u.
  • Sadarkaa qindoomina lubbuu (atoms irraa hanga biosphere) ibsuu ni danda'u.

2. Seensa (Introduction)

Seeliin wantoota lubbuu qaban keessaa xiqqaa fi bu'uura isaati. Organiizimoonni seelii tokko qaban (fkn: baakteeriyaa) ykn seelii hedduu qaban (fkn: biqiltuu, nama) jedhamanii ramadamu. Argannoon seelii maaykirooskooppii uumamuun salphaa ta'e.

3. Yaadannoo Barnootaa (Detailed Notes)

Kutaa 1: Yaada Seelii (Cell Theory)

  • Robert Hooke (1665): Qorqorroo ilaalee maqaa "seelii" kenne.
  • Antonie van Leeuwenhoek (1674): Seelii lubbuu qaban ilaalee "animalcules" jedhee waame.
  • Matthias Schleiden (1838): Biqiltoonni hundi seelii irraa ijaaraman jedhee dhiyeesse.
  • Theodor Schwann (1839): Lubbuun hundi seelii irraa ijaarama jedhee mirkaneesse.
  • Rudolf Virchow (1856): Seeliin hundi seelii duraan ture irraa uumama ("Omnis cellula e cellula").

Yaadota Ijoo: 1) Lubbuun hundi seelii tokko ykn isaa ol irraa ijaarama. 2) Seeliin bu'uura caasaa fi hojii lubbuu ti. 3) Seeliin hundi seelii duraan ture irraa uumama.

Kutaa 2: Caasaa fi Hojii Seelii (Organelles)

  • Dallaa Seelii (Cell Wall): Deeggarsa kennu. Biqiltoota, faanjii, algae fi baakteeriyaa keessatti argama. Bineensota keessatti hin argamu.
  • Membraana Seelii (Cell Membrane): Wantoota seelii keessa seenanii fi ba'an to'atu.
  • Saayitooplaazimii (Cytoplasm): Dhangala'aa (70-80% bishaan) sochii keemikaalaa qabu.
  • Niwkiliyasii (Nucleus): Organelle guddaa. Chromosomes (DNA) qaba. Addaan ba'uu seelii to'ata. Nucleolus (ribosomes hojjetu) of keessatti qabata.
  • Raayboosoomii (Ribosomes): Pirootiinii hojjetu.
  • Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER):
    - Rough ER: Raayboosoomii qaba; pirootiinii hojjeta.
    - Smooth ER: Raayboosoomii hin qabu; liipiidii hojjeta.
  • Vesicles: Wantoota geejjibu.
  • Golgi Apparatus: Pirootiinii fi liipiidii qulqulleessee raabsa.
  • Maayitookondiriyaa (Mitochondria): "Humna seelii." ATP (humna) oomisha.
  • Lysosomes: Enzaayimii bulbuluu qabu, baakteeriyaa fi wantoota faayidaa hin qabne diigu.
  • Vacuoles: Kuusaa. Seelii biqiltuu keessatti bal'aa; bineensa keessatti xiqqaa.
  • Chloroplasts: Bakka photosynthesis (chlorophyll qaba). Biqiltoota qofa keessa jira.
  • Centrioles & Microtubules: Addaan ba'uu seelii gargaaru. Centrioles seelii bineensa qofa keessa jira.
  • Cilia & Flagella: Sochii (locomotion) ofii raawwachuuf (Cilia = gabaabaa/baay'ee, Flagella = dheeraa/muraasa).

Kutaa 3: Gosa Seelii

  • Prokaryotic: "Niwkiliyasii dura." Xiqqaa (1-10 μm), organelles membraana qaban hin qabu, DNA geengoo. Fkn: Baakteeriyaa.
  • Eukaryotic: "Niwkiliyasii dhugaa." Guddaa (10-100 μm), organelles membraana qaban qaba. Fkn: Biqiltuu, Bineensa.

Kutaa 4: Seelii Biqiltuu fi Bineensaa

  • Seelii Biqiltuu: Dallaa seelii jabaa, chloroplasts, vacuole guddaa dhaabbataa.
  • Seelii Bineensaa: Boca wal hin fakkaanne, centrioles, lysosomes, dallaa seelii hin qabu, vacuole xiqqaa qaba.

Kutaa 5: Seelii Ilaaluu fi Transport

  • Staining: Iodine (seelii qullaa/biqiltuu), Methylene Blue (seelii bineensa/cheek).
  • Passive Transport: Humna hin barbaadu.
    - Diffusion: Olkaa irraa gara gadiitti.
    - Osmosis: Bishaan membraana keessa darbu.
  • Active Transport: Gadi irraa gara olkaatti (membraana faallaa) ATP fayyadamuun socho'u.
  • Tonicity:
    - Hypertonic: Bishaan seelii keessaa ba'a (seeliin xiqqaata/gogti).
    - Isotonic: Bishaan walqixa socho'a.
    - Hypotonic: Bishaan seelii keessa seena (seeliin dhiita'a ykn turgid ta'a).

Kutaa 6: Sadarkaalee Qindoomina Lubbuu

Atoms → Molecules → Organelles → Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ System → Organism → Population → Community → Ecosystem → Biosphere.

5. Cuunfaa (Summary)

  • Seeliin bu'uura lubbuu ti, Yaadni Seelii (Cell Theory) kana mirkaneessa.
  • Seelii Eukaryotic keessatti organelles adda addaa (akka mitochondria fi nucleus) hojii addaa raawwatu qaba.
  • Seelii biqiltuu fi bineensaa amaloota waliinii qabaatanis, caasaa akka dallaa seelii fi chloroplasts adda ba'u.
  • Seeliin naannoo isaa waliin diffusion, osmosis fi active transport fayyadamuun hariiroo qaba.
  • Lubbuun sadarkaa atoms irraa hanga biosphere qindaa'a.
