1. What is a Cell?

Cell is the smallest and the basic unit of living things. Organisms are classified as unicellular or multicellular based on the number of cells.

  • Unicellular: Consist of a single cell. Can live independently (e.g., Bacteria, protozoa, yeast).
  • Multicellular: Composed of many specialized cells that work together (e.g., Humans, plants).

2. Cell Theory

  • Robert Hooke (1665): Observed cork and coined the term "cell."
  • Antonie van Leeuwenhoek (1674): Observed living cells (bacteria, protozoa) and called them "animalcules."
  • Matthias Schleiden (1838): Proposed all plants are made of cells.
  • Theodor Schwann (1839): Extended to animals; all living organisms are composed of cells.
  • Rudolf Virchow (1856): All cells arise from pre-existing cells ("Omnis cellula e cellula").

Three Core Tenets: 1) All living organisms are composed of one or more cells. 2) The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. 3) All cells arise from pre-existing cells.

3. Cell Structure and Function (Organelles)

  • Cell Wall: Rigid structure for support. Found in plant, fungal, algal, and bacterial cells. Absent in animals.
  • Cell Membrane: Thin, selectively permeable outer covering controlling what enters/leaves.
  • Cytoplasm: Thick fluid (70-80% water) where chemical reactions occur and organelles are suspended.
  • Nucleus: Largest organelle, contains chromosomes (DNA), controls cell division. Contains the Nucleolus (makes ribosomes).
  • Ribosomes: Small structures (free or on RER) that synthesize proteins.
  • Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER):
    - Rough ER (RER): Studded with ribosomes; synthesizes and transports proteins.
    - Smooth ER (SER): Lacks ribosomes; synthesizes lipids.
  • Vesicles: Small sacs for storing and transporting materials.
  • Golgi Apparatus: Modifies, packages, and distributes proteins/lipids.
  • Mitochondria: "Powerhouse of the cell." Double membrane; produces energy (ATP) via respiration.
  • Lysosomes: Sacs with digestive enzymes to break down bacteria/waste.
  • Vacuoles: Single membrane. Plant cells have large central vacuoles (maintain turgor). Animal cells have small ones. (Includes Food and Contractile vacuoles).
  • Chloroplasts: Double membrane; site of photosynthesis; contains green chlorophyll (plants only).
  • Centrioles & Microtubules: Assist in cell division (spindle fibers). Centrioles are specific to animal cells.
  • Cilia & Flagella: For locomotion. Cilia (short, many), Flagella (long, few).

4. Types of Cells

  • Prokaryotic: "Before nucleus". Small (1-10 μm), no membrane-bound organelles. DNA circular. Cell wall present (peptidoglycan). E.g., Bacteria.
  • Eukaryotic: "True nucleus". Larger (10-100 μm), has membrane-bound organelles. DNA linear. Plant cell wall present; animal absent. E.g., Plants, Animals.

5. Plant vs. Animal Cells

Plant Cells: Larger, rigid cell wall, chloroplasts, large permanent vacuole.

Animal Cells: Irregular shape, contain centrioles and lysosomes, no cell wall, no chloroplasts, small temporary vacuoles.

6. Observing Cells Under a Microscope

Stains: Iodine (for onion/plant cells), Methylene Blue (for cheek/animal cells).

Steps: Prepare thin slices, stain, start with lowest magnification (e.g., 4x), focus using coarse knob, then switch to higher power using fine knob.

7. The Cell and Its Environment (Transport)

  • Passive Transport: No energy needed.
    - Diffusion: High to low concentration. Rate depends on concentration gradient, temperature, mass, distance, and surface area.
    - Osmosis: Movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane.
  • Active Transport: Movement from low to high concentration (against gradient) requiring energy (ATP). Example: Root hairs absorbing ions, intestine absorbing glucose.

Osmosis Solutions:

  • Hypertonic: High solute outside. Water leaves cell. Plant cell plasmolysis (wilts); Animal cell shrinks.
  • Isotonic: Equal solute. No net water movement.
  • Hypotonic: Low solute outside. Water enters cell. Plant cell becomes turgid; Animal cell swells/bursts (Hemolysis).

8. Levels of Biological Organization

Atoms → Molecules → Organelles → Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ System → Organism → Population → Community → Ecosystem → Biosphere.

1. Seeliin Maali?

Seeliin wantoota lubbuu qaban keessaa xiqqaa fi bu'uura isaati. Organiizimoonni baay'ina seelii isaaniitiin seelii tokko (unicellular) ykn seelii hedduu (multicellular) jedhamanii ramadamu.

  • Seelii Tokko (Unicellular): Seelii tokko qofa irraa ijaaraman. Of danda'anii jiraachuu danda'u (fkn: Baakteeriyaa, protozoa, raayistii).
  • Seelii Hedduu (Multicellular): Seelii adda addaa baay'ee irraa ijaaramanii hojii waliin hojjetan (fkn: Nama, biqiltuu).

2. Yaada Seelii (Cell Theory)

  • Robert Hooke (1665): Qorqorroo (cork) maaykirooskooppiin ilaalee, maqaa "seelii" jedhu kenne.
  • Antonie van Leeuwenhoek (1674): Seelii lubbuu qaban (baakteeriyaa, protozoa) ilaalee "animalcules" jedhee waame.
  • Matthias Schleiden (1838): Biqiltoonni hundi seelii irraa ijaaraman jedhee dhiyeesse.
  • Theodor Schwann (1839): Bineensota irrattis hojiin Schleiden babal'ate; lubbuun hundi seelii irraa ijaarama.
  • Rudolf Virchow (1856): Seeliin hundi seelii duraan ture irraa uumama ("Omnis cellula e cellula").

Yaadota Ijoo Sadii: 1) Lubbuun hundi seelii tokko ykn isaa ol irraa ijaarama. 2) Seeliin bu'uura caasaa fi hojii lubbuu ti. 3) Seeliin hundi seelii duraan ture irraa uumama.

3. Caasaa fi Hojii Seelii (Organelles)

  • Dallaa Seelii (Cell Wall): Caasaa jabaa deeggarsa kennu. Biqiltoota, faanjii, algae fi baakteeriyaa keessatti argama. Bineensota keessatti hin argamu.
  • Membraana Seelii (Cell Membrane): Qola qallaa, wantoota seelii keessa seenanii fi ba'an to'atu.
  • Saayitooplaazimii (Cytoplasm): Dhangala'aa (70-80% bishaan) sochii keemikaalaa fi organelles qabu.
  • Niwkiliyasii (Nucleus): Organelle guddaa, chromosomes (DNA) qabu, addaan ba'uu seelii to'ata. Nucleolus (ribosomes hojjetu) of keessatti qabata.
  • Raayboosoomii (Ribosomes): Caasaa xiqqaa pirootiinii hojjetu (bilisaa ykn RER irratti argama).
  • Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER):
    - Rough ER (RER): Raayboosoomii qaba; pirootiinii hojjete transport godha.
    - Smooth ER (SER): Raayboosoomii hin qabu; liipiidii (lipid) hojjeta.
  • Vesicles: Korojoo xiqqaa wantoota kuusuu fi geejjibuuf gargaaru.
  • Golgi Apparatus: Pirootiinii fi liipiidii qulqulleessee, paakijii godhee raabsa.
  • Maayitookondiriyaa (Mitochondria): "Humna seelii." Membraana lama qaba; sochii respirationiin ATP (humna) oomisha.
  • Lysosomes: Korojoo enzaayimii bulbuluu qabu, baakteeriyaa fi wantoota faayidaa hin qabne diigu.
  • Vacuoles: Membraana tokko qaba. Seelii biqiltuu keessatti bal'aa (turgor eega). Seelii bineensa keessatti xiqqaa. (Food fi Contractile vacuoles ni dabalata).
  • Chloroplasts: Membraana lama qaba; bakka photosynthesis; chlorophyll magariisa qaba (biqiltoota qofa).
  • Centrioles & Microtubules: Addaan ba'uu seelii gargaaru. Centrioles seelii bineensa qofa keessa jira.
  • Cilia & Flagella: Sochii (locomotion) ofii raawwachuuf. Cilia (gabaabaa, baay'ee), Flagella (dheeraa, muraasa).

4. Gosa Seelii

  • Prokaryotic: "Niwkiliyasii dura." Xiqqaa (1-10 μm), organelles membraana qaban hin qabu. DNA geengoo. Dallaa seelii qaba (peptidoglycan). Fkn: Baakteeriyaa.
  • Eukaryotic: "Niwkiliyasii dhugaa." Guddaa (10-100 μm), organelles membraana qaban qaba. DNA sararaawaa. Biqiltuun dallaa qaba; bineensi hin qabu. Fkn: Biqiltuu, Bineensa.

5. Seelii Biqiltuu fi Bineensaa

Seelii Biqiltuu: Guddaa, dallaa seelii jabaa, chloroplasts, vacuole guddaa dhaabbataa.

Seelii Bineensaa: Boca wal hin fakkaanne, centrioles fi lysosomes qaba, dallaa seelii hin qabu, chloroplasts hin qabu, vacuole xiqqaa qaba.

6. Maaykirooskooppiin Seelii Ilaaluu

Stain (Halluu): Iodine (seelii qullaa/biqiltuu), Methylene Blue (seelii bineensa/cheek).

Tarkaanfiiwwan: Seelii qopheessi, halluu itti naqi, magnification xixiqqaan (fkn: 4x) jalqabi, coarse knob fayyadami, booda gara guddichaatti ce'ii fine knob fayyadami.

7. Seelii fi Naannoo Isaa (Transport)

  • Passive Transport: Humna hin barbaadu.
    - Diffusion: Konkeentireeshinii olkaa irraa gara gadiitti. Saffisni isaa gradient, ho'ina, ulfina, fageenya fi bal'ina irratti hundaa'a.
    - Osmosis: Bishaan membraana keessa darbuun olkaa irraa gara gadiitti.
  • Active Transport: Konkeentireeshinii gadi irraa gara olkaatti (membraana faallaa) humna (ATP) barbaachisuun socho'u. Fkn: Hiddi nyaata xuuxuu.

Sirna Osmosis:

  • Hypertonic: Solute naannoo keessaa baay'ee. Bishaan seelii keessaa ba'a. Biqiltuun gogti (plasmolysis); bineensi xiqqaata.
  • Isotonic: Solute walqixa. Bishaan hin socho'u.
  • Hypotonic: Solute naannoo keessaa xiqqaa. Bishaan seelii keessa seena. Biqiltuun dhaabbataa (turgid) ta'a; bineensi dhiita'a (burst/Hemolysis).
